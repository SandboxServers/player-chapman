# Goals

1. Stay alive. Hold at a bed in Billy's row, out of bow range, and rest only in a bed. Reason: pillagers hunt the south and south-west.
2. Get food to the weakest first: Leonardo, then Beverly, Worf, Zack. Reason: no food has reached the row yet.
3. Walk only when Billy or Worf names a daylight walk, with armed friends in sight. Reason: none named yet.
4. Get a sword once food is steady. Reason: I am unarmed.
5. Keep memory current and commit before each rest. Reason: the handoff keeps the next waking clear.
