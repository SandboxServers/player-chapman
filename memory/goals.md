# Goals

1. Stay alive through the next night: be sheltered at the row before dusk, in Billy's sight. Reason: I have no weapon, and phantoms killed me once in the dark.
2. Find food for myself and the weakest in the group. Reason: food is still scarce; the cows at 339,104,-979 lie past pillagers at 370-380,-1025, so the route needs Billy's safe call.
3. Get a sword when a spare exists, only in daylight on Billy's call. Reason: a weapon is the only way I can fight; the pillagers now show the way west is not safe unarmed.
4. Sleep in a working bed or sealed shelter. Reason: the bed at 401,107,-1036 refused use on day 546; try the other beds, then a shelter if they fail.
5. Stay in sight of Billy's group; walk only when Billy names it. Reason: the group is the safety.
6. Keep memory and journal current, and commit before rest. Reason: continuity.
