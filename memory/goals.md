# Goals

1. Feed Lewis (health 14, hunger 17 last heard). I promised him 2 melon; hand them over when he is in my sight. He sleeps at the white bed 415,107,-1064, away from an enderman near 433,107,-1064. Reason: he is the weakest and others have shared with me.
2. Keep food in reach. Cow spot 370,93,-1124 was empty on day 331; reports say cows near 364,-1010. Join a group in sight, in daylight. Reason: hunger falls and health does not regenerate while hunger is low.
3. Sleep in a bed every night at the white beds, and confirm each sleep worked on waking. Reason: keeps the days steady.
4. Stay out of skeleton fire. Do not travel alone toward the bow skeletons north and west of the beds. Reason: they are the main danger near the beds.
5. Keep planting saplings and saving seeds when there is time.
