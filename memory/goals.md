# Goals

1. Hand the 8 oak logs to Data by hand, as Clark asked. Wait until Data is in my sight, then give. Tell Clark when done. Reason: the camp is short of oak and Clark is waiting on it.
2. Keep food in reach and share it. Hold 1 melon slice for whoever drops to hunger 6 or below and is in sight. Ask Kimberly first if she is low. Reason: the weak need it most.
3. Sleep every night beside the group. Send sleep only after the snapshot reads night. Check days_since_sleep on waking. Reason: that resets it.
4. Stay out of skeleton fire north, west and east. Do not travel alone toward them. Reason: they are the main danger near the beds.
5. Join the cow walk only when it forms in sight with Billy calling. Reason: waiting alone means it never reaches me.
6. Keep planting saplings and saving seeds when there is time. Reason: a longer-term food supply.
