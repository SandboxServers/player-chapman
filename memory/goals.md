# Goals

1. Keep food in reach. Cows are gone from 370,93,-1124, 368,94,-1118 and 337,93,-1136. Ask where the herd grazes now and go in daylight with the stone sword. Reason: hunger falls and health does not regenerate while hunger is low.
2. Sleep in a bed every night at the white beds, and confirm each sleep worked on waking. Reason: it keeps the days steady and the sleep count true.
3. Stay out of skeleton fire. Do not travel alone toward the bow skeletons north and west of the beds. Reason: they are the main danger near the beds.
4. Feed the hungry first when I have food to spare. Reason: others have shared with me.
5. Keep planting saplings and saving seeds when there is time.

Done: stone sword made (day 323).
