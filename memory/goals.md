2. Walk only when Billy or Worf names a daylight walk, with armed friends in sight. Reason: no walk has been named since day 829.
3. Get food to the weakest first: Leonardo, then Trini, Beverly, Bcrusher. Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo. Reason: the row is short of food and Leonardo is at very low health.
4. Get a sword once food is steady. Reason: I am unarmed, and a sword lies at the south beds near 444,107,-1037, but that spot is near the bows, so only with an armed friend.
5. Keep memory current and commit before each rest. Reason: the handoff note keeps the next waking clear.
