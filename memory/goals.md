# Goals

1. Stay alive: sleep at night at bed 443,107,-1023 or -1022 in Billy's row. Reason: bows are southwest, and I died once walking alone.
2. Hold at the bed until Billy names a daylight walk. Never walk alone under bows. Reason: the row moves together or not at all.
3. Give food first to the weakest: Leonardo, then Trini. Eat when Billy or Bcrusher hands out potatoes. Reason: they are weaker than I am.
4. Do not walk west of x=370 or south toward the bows.
5. Get a sword into my pack once food is steady.
6. Keep memory and journal current, and commit before rest.
