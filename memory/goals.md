# Goals

1. Give 4 wheat to Laforge when he is in sight, so he can make bread. Reason: promised, and he has no food.
2. Keep watch for Data; when he is in sight, tell Clark (who holds his 8 oak logs). Reason: the camp is short of oak.
3. Keep clear of pillagers to the south (around 413,-1045) and skeleton fire north, west and east. Reason: Billy and Laforge were killed near there.
4. Hold 1 melon slice or wheat-bread for whoever drops to hunger 6 or below and is in sight; Tommy is at health 2, no food. Reason: the weak need it most.
5. Sleep beside the group each night. Reason: resets days_since_sleep.
6. Repay the meals given by Worf, MacDonald, Picard and Beverly when they are low. Reason: I owe them.
7. Join Billy's cow walk only if someone calls it and it forms in sight. Reason: waiting alone never reaches me. (Billy is dead, so this may need a new leader.)
8. Plant saplings and save seeds when there is time. Reason: longer-term food.

Done: oak logs handed to Clark on day 410. MacDonald's 4 wheat received on day 413.
