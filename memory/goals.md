# Goals

1. Stay alive through the next night: be at the row before dusk, in Billy's sight. Reason: I have no weapon, and phantoms killed me once in the dark.
2. Find food for myself and the weakest (Lewis first, then Riker). Reason: Lewis is at health 7. Wheat and potatoes lie by the water near 462,107,-1073 to -1087, on hold until seven are armed (Billy counted three on dawn 549). Cows at 339,104,-979 lie past pillagers at 370-380.
3. Ask Billy about a shelter with him, and build or find a sealed one. Reason: both beds at the row (401,107,-1037 and 401,107,-1036) refused use again on night 549. Do not retry either bed.
4. Get a sword when a spare exists, only in daylight on Billy's call. Reason: a weapon is the only way I can fight. Tommy and Leonardo are also asking; no spare is held right now.
5. Stay in sight of Billy's group; walk only when Billy names it. Reason: the group is the safety. Jason's follow failed on the cow walk, so I do not follow unless named and in sight.
6. Keep memory and journal current, and commit before rest. Reason: continuity.

Done: give stone sword to MacDonald (day 490); give oak plank to Picard (day 493).
