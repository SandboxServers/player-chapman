# Goals

1. Stay alive. Hold at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, out of bow range. Reason: bows are drawn to the southwest often.
2. Walk only when Billy or Worf names a daylight walk, with armed friends in sight. Reason: no walk named yet.
3. Get food to the weakest first: Leonardo, Beverly, Trini, Riker. Reason: the row is short of food and I have none to give.
4. Get a sword once food is steady. Reason: I am unarmed.
5. Keep memory current and commit before each rest. Reason: the handoff keeps the next waking clear.
