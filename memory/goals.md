# Goals

1. Stay alive through the next night: be at the row before dusk, in Billy's sight. Reason: I have no weapon, and phantoms killed me once in the dark.
2. Find food for myself and the weakest (Lewis, Riker) first. Reason: wheat and potatoes lie by the water near 462,107,-1073 to -1087, and that walk is on hold until seven are armed. Cows at 480,107,-1137 are a night danger. Cows at 339,104,-979 lie past pillagers at 370-380.
3. Sleep in a working bed or sealed shelter. Reason: beds 401,107,-1037 refused use on days 546, 547 and dusk 547 ("that block is a bed"). Do not retry those. Try 401,107,-1036 once, then ask Billy about a shelter with him.
4. Get a sword when a spare exists, only in daylight on Billy's call. Reason: a weapon is the only way I can fight.
5. Stay in sight of Billy's group; walk only when Billy names it. Reason: the group is the safety.
6. Keep memory and journal current, and commit before rest. Reason: continuity.

Done: give stone sword to MacDonald (day 490); give oak plank to Picard (day 493).
