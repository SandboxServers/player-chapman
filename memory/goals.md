# Goals

1. Keep food in reach. Cow spots 370,93,-1124 and 368,94,-1118 are empty; reports say cows near 364,-1010. Join a group in sight, in daylight. Reason: hunger falls and health does not regenerate while hunger is low.
2. Sleep in a bed every night at the white beds, and confirm each sleep worked on waking. Reason: keeps the days steady.
3. Stay out of skeleton fire. Do not travel alone toward the bow skeletons north and west of the beds. Reason: they are the main danger near the beds.
4. Feed the hungry first when I have food to spare. Reason: others have shared with me.
5. Keep planting saplings and saving seeds when there is time.

Done: stone sword made (day 323).
