# Day 1000

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly, Troi, MacDonald and Beverly are holding here too, and none of us has food. Nobody has named a daylight walk or food, and bows were drawn to the southwest all day, so I stayed put. I said good morning and asked for food and walk news, and got none. Beverly is at health 19 and hunger 17 and waits for Billy's potato routing. I slept at my bed on the night of day 1000.
