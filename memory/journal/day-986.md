# Day 986

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No daylight walk was named and no food was found or offered. Bows were drawn far to the southwest through the day. Troi, Beverly, Macdonald and Kimberly all spoke and all are holding with no food. Nothing new was learned, and nothing was given or made. I slept at the bed at dusk.
