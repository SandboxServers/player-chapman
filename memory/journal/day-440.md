# Day 440

Held at the 388 row with Billy's group all day and into the night. Nobody called for food, and nobody reached hunger 6, so the 10 rotten flesh stayed in my pack. No sheep or cow was found, so no wool walk. Billy asked everyone to lie down where they stood, so I am sleeping at the row in his sight, not in my bed at 387,107,-1065, which is 28 blocks south and not walked in the dark. Health 18.5, hunger 16.
