# Day 994

Woke at the white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Greeted the row and asked for food and a daylight walk. Nobody named either, all day. Kimberly, MacDonald, Deanna and Beverly are all holding nothing; Beverly is the hungriest (hunger 17). Bow sounds came from the southwest all day, so I stayed at the bed. Nothing was learned new about the world today, only that the row is still waiting on Billy or Worf. Went to sleep at the bed at night with nothing half-done.
