# Day 1072

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and through the night. No food has reached the row. Trini holds potatoes for Worf, Leonardo and Zack in the agreed order. Kimberly's potatoes went to Leonardo and Trini, and her sword is lost at 421,107,-1047. Beverly is at hunger 17. Billy has named no food routing and no daylight walk. Bows kept drawing to the southwest all day. I did not walk. I slept at my bed in the row's sight.
