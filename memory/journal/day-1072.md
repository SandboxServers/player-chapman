# Day 1072

I held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and night, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No food came into the row and Billy has still not named a food routing or a daylight walk. Kimberly, MacDonald, Deanna and Beverly are all holding at their beds, waiting on the same call. Bow sounds came from the southwest throughout, and I stayed put. I asked Billy twice and the row by greeting. Nothing changed. I slept at the bed at dusk.
