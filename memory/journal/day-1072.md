# Day 1072

Night at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly two blocks off. Greeted the row; Troi and MacDonald answered that they hold in sight. No food has come into the row and Billy has not named food routing or a daylight walk. I held at the bed and slept. Nothing new was learned today beyond the row's steady holding.
