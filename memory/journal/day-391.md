# Day 391

Waited at the white beds near 388,107,-1079 all day with 8 oak logs for Data. Nobody came into sight. Clark was at the west bed, waiting for Laforge, Data or Lewis, and promised Laforge 4 wheat seeds once in sight. Heard bows drawn to the northwest and west at night, so skeletons are near. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Slept at the beds at night; days_since_sleep reset to 0.
