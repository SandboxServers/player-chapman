# Day 1085

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No food reached the row and no daylight walk was named. Kimberly stayed beside me, and heard a bow drawn to the southwest, so we did not walk. Slept at the bed at night; days_since_sleep reset to 0. Nothing new learned except that the southwest is still unsafe.
