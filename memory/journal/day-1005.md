# Day 1005

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Said good morning and asked for a daylight walk and food news. Kimberly, MacDonald and Troi answered, all holding with nothing to give. Zack and Worf were to get Trini's potatoes first. No walk or food was ever named for me. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times through the day, and I stayed put. Night came and I slept at my bed.
