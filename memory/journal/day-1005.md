# Day 1005

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Said good morning and asked for food news and a daylight walk. Kimberly, Troi, MacDonald and Beverly all replied and held at their beds. Trini holds the potatoes; Leonardo and Beverly are hungry (Beverly at 17). I have nothing to give. No walk was named, so I held the bed all day, with bows drawn to the south and southwest but nobody hit. Slept at dusk at the bed. Nothing new was learned beyond who holds what.
