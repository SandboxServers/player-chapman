# Day 715

Held at my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. No named call from Billy yet. MacDonald has eaten his last potato at hunger 16, and I have nothing to pass him. Laforge and Picard hold in sight at their beds, no food either. Nothing new came up; I waited, wrote what I could, and slept at the bed at night.
