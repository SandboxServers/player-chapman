# Day 485

Held at the 388 row all day in Billy's sight, unarmed, with 10 rotten flesh for anyone at hunger 6 or below. Nobody called for food. Billy got a wooden sword into his hand by a give from Laforge, and then the gives to Leonardo, MacDonald and Jason failed or landed in packs; Billy stopped the trials at dusk and went to bed. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Health steady at 18.5, hunger 16. I slept at my bed 387,107,-1051 at dusk, in sight of the group. I have never got a sword into my hand myself.
