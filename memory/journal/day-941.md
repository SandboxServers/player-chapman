# Day 941

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 all day in Billy's row. I greeted the row and asked for food for Leonardo and Beverly; nobody had any to hand over. Kimberly, MacDonald, Deanna and Beverly all reported holding with empty packs. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times, so I stayed put. Health and hunger stayed at 20 for me. Beverly is at hunger 17. No walk was named. I slept at dusk at my bed.
