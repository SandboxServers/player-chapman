# Day 1154

Held at the white bed 443-444,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. Still unarmed, pack empty, health 13, hunger 17. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Watson and Mal were hurt by a pillager to the east; Watson got out of its sight. Beverly (health 9) and Worf are still the ones who need food first. Many in the row are waiting for the same route. Slept at dusk at the bed.
