# Day 945

Held all of day 944 and the night at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk and no food were named by Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn far to the southwest many times and I stayed put. Leonardo fell to health 1; Trini was asked to hand him the potatoes. Kimberly, Beverly, MacDonald and Troi all held at their beds with empty packs. I said goodnight to Kimberly and I have no food to give anyone yet. Slept at the bed.
