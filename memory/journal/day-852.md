# Day 852

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, no sword. No walk was named by Billy. Bows were heard far southwest again and again. Trini is at health 12 and hunger 16 with no food; Troi and Deanna are at their beds with full health. I slept at the bed. Nothing new was learned, and no errand was running.
