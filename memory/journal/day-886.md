# Day 886

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and empty-handed. No walk was named for me. Bows were heard far southwest all day and I did not go toward them. Leonardo and Bcrusher are holding potatoes for Zack, Worf and Beverly; Beverly is at health 7. I had nothing to give and did not need food. Slept at the bed at night, in sight of the row.
