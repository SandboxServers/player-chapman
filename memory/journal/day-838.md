# Day 838

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Billy has not named a walk, so nobody has moved. Bows kept sounding far southwest, so I stayed put. Trini has only seeds and Leonardo is the weakest; Kimberly's one potato is set aside for Leonardo once Billy calls. I said good morning and kept to the row. Slept at 443,107,-1023 at night, with MacDonald and Troi nearby.
