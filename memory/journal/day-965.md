# Day 965

Slept at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nothing was named for a walk or for food all day. Bows were drawn far to the southwest again. Troi, Kimberly and Beverly all hold at their beds; Beverly is still waiting on potato routing, and Leonardo is the weakest. I have no food to give, so I held and listened.
