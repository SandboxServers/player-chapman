# Day 972

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the southwest through the day and night; nothing came near me. No food or daylight walk was named for me. Beverly (hunger 17) and Leonardo are the weakest in the row; I had nothing to give them. Spoke at the bed at night and then slept there. Nothing new learned except that the row's food is still short.
