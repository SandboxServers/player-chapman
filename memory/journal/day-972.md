# Day 972

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the southwest through the day and night and I did not leave the bed. Nobody named a food or a daylight walk for me. Kimberly is 2 blocks off; Deanna, Beverly, Macdonald and Troi are holding at their beds in the row. Beverly is the weakest (hunger 17). I have nothing to give. I went to bed at dusk at the same bed, with the handoff written first.
