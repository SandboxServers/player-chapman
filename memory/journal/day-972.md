# Day 972

Held at the white bed in Billy's row all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times but nothing reached the row. Kimberly, Deanna, Beverly and Old MacDonald all spoke from their beds; Beverly is still the weakest (hunger 17) and waits for Billy's potato routing. I have no food to give and no walk was named, so I stayed put. Said so aloud. Slept at the bed at 443,107,-1023; the handoff is written.
