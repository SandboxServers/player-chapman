# Day 1098

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. No food reached anyone, and no daylight walk or call came from Billy or Worf. Leonardo is at health 1 with hunger 17, and Beverly is at hunger 17. Kimberly, MacDonald and Deanna are holding with full health and empty packs. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times, so I stayed put. I greeted the row, said I had no food, and waited. Night came and I slept at my bed beside Kimberly.
