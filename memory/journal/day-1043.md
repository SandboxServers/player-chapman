# Day 1043 (night)

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Greeted Kimberly, who stayed two blocks off. Troi, Beverly and MacDonald each said no food and no daylight walk had been named yet. Troi reported Worf at hunger 7 and Leonardo still at health 1. Bows were drawn to the southwest several times, and I stayed out of range. Nothing was given or carried. I said goodnight to Kimberly and slept at my bed.
