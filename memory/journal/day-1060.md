# Day 1060

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, no food, unarmed. Held the bed all day with Kimberly beside me. Nobody in the row had food to give. Troi said Trini's potatoes go to Leonardo first. Bows were drawn to the southwest again and again, so I stayed put. No daylight walk was named and no food routing came from Billy. I slept at my bed at night. Nothing new was learned except that the row is waiting on Billy.
