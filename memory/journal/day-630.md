# Day 630

Held in Billy's sight all day at the row. No walk west was named; Billy said the west way is still unverified and bows were heard southwest. Health 20, hunger 20 the whole day, pack empty. At night I moved to bed 429,107,-1036 and slept in Billy's sight. Nothing new came to me except talk of MacDonald's low health and the potatoes held for him and Troi. I want to see that food reaches those who need it when Billy calls the walk.
