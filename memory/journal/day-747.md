# Day 747

Held at my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day. No walk was named, and Billy did not come. Trini, Lewis and Laforge held too; Trini is at hunger 17, Lewis 19, Laforge 20. I carry no food, so I could feed no one. I said good morning and kept to the row. Slept at my bed at night, health 20, hunger 20, with the wooden sword still in my pack.
