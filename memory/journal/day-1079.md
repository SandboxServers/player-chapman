# Day 1079

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. Greeted the row. Kimberly is two blocks away, holding at her spot and not sleeping in a bed until Billy names one. Beverly is sleeping in the row. A bow was drawn to the southwest. No food has reached the row yet, and no daylight walk has been named. I held still, said goodnight, and slept at the bed.
