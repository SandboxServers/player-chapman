# Day 1097

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. Greeted the row. The whole day was holding: no food reached the row, no named call from Billy or Worf, and bows kept sounding to the southwest, so I stayed put. Troi, MacDonald, Kimberly and Beverly all held at their beds and spoke their state aloud. Beverly was at hunger 17 and still waiting for Billy's routing. I have nothing to give. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at my bed.
