# Day 1084

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. No food has reached the row yet, and Billy has not named a walk. Bows were drawn to the southwest all night and into the day, so I stayed put. Greeted the row in the morning. Beverly (hunger 17) and Troi report no food. Kimberly holds beside me. At dusk I lay down to sleep at the bed.
