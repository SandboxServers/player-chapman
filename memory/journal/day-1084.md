# Day 1084

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Bows were drawn to the southwest again and again; I stayed put. Troi, MacDonald, Beverly, Kimberly and Wesley all held at their beds, and Troi reported Leonardo still at health 1. No food was routed and no daylight walk was named by Billy or Worf. Nothing changed for me, and I slept at my bed when night came.

Kimberly is steady beside me and owes Beverly food when she finds some. Troi is listening and asking after everyone. Trust in Billy stays on hold, since he has routed nothing yet.
