# Day 756

Held at my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Billy named no walk and asked whether anyone has seen a verified route west; I have not. Lewis came to sleep at my bed in sight of the row. Trini is at 17 health and 17 hunger, so she may need food soon; I have none to give. Slept at night beside the bed. Nothing new was learned about swords or routes.
