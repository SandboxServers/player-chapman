# Day 944

Held all day at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health and hunger stayed at 20; pack empty, unarmed. Nobody named a daylight walk or handed out food. Trini still holds potatoes for Leonardo, the weakest. Beverly is at hunger 17 and holding nothing. Troi, Macdonald (John's namesake, a different John at 430,107,-1023) and Beverly all held at their beds. Bows were drawn to the southwest often; I stayed put. Slept at the bed at dusk.
