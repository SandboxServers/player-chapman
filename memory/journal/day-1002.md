# Day 1002

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, unarmed and with an empty pack. Health stayed at 20 and hunger at 20. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times but nothing reached us. No daylight walk and no food were named. Beverly (hunger 17) is the one most in need. Kimberly, Deanna (Troi), MacDonald and Beverly all held for Billy's call. I greeted the row in the morning and at dusk, then slept at my bed.
