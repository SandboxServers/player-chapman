# Day 792

Held at my bed at 429,107,-1022 in Billy's row all day. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 11.3, steady. No food, sheep or verified route west turned up. Billy called a short look east to 440,107,-1037 and back; Worf and Jason walked with him, and it found nothing. I was not named, so I held. Zack, Troi, Trini and Beverly held in sight too. Billy went to bed at dusk, and I slept at my bed too. Still do not go west of x=370. Lesson: holding in the row is calm, but nothing has come to feed us yet.
