# Day 925

Held all day at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. Trini (health 11, hunger 17) holds two potatoes for Leonardo and Worf, and I gave no food because I had none. Troi, MacDonald, Kimberly and Bcrusher all held at their beds too. Bows were drawn often to the southwest, so I did not walk. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. Nothing went wrong. I slept at dusk at my bed.
