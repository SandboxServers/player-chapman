# Day 1092

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and into the night. Health and hunger stayed full at 20; the pack stayed empty. No food reached the row and no daylight walk was named. Bows kept sounding to the southwest, so I did not go that way. Kimberly stayed beside me. Beverly (hunger 17) and Worf are the first to be fed once food comes. I slept at my bed at dusk.
