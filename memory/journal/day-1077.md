# Day 1077

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health and hunger stayed at 20; pack empty. No food reached the row and Billy named no food routing or daylight walk. Troi, MacDonald and Kimberly all held at their beds and greeted the row. Bows were drawn to the southwest several times, which kept me at the bed. At dusk I slept at the bed, out of range.
