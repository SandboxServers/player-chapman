# Day 1141

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, and no food reached anyone. Health stayed at 13 and hunger at 17. Riker, Kimberly, Lewis, Trini and Beverly are holding the same way; Beverly has health 9 and is the weakest. Trini found no food in the chest at 433,107,-1051. I said aloud where I stand and asked who has seen the potatoes at 464-469,107,-1073. Nothing was confirmed. I slept at the bed at dusk.
