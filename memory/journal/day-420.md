# Day 420

Gave my 3 wheat to Lewis on the morning of 419, and he made bread for himself. I have had no food since. Looked for Data all day to hand him the 8 oak logs that Clark holds for him; Data has not been seen. Clark had bread and Laforge was bringing him bread. At dusk I walked to the 401 beds and went to sleep among the group. Health steady at about 13.6, hunger 17.
