# Day 937

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Kimberly stayed two blocks off. Bows were drawn again and again far to the southwest, and I stayed out of their reach. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. Beverly, Trini and Riker are short of food; I had none to give. Slept at the bed at night.
