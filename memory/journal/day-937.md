# Day 937

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn far to the southwest many times. Kimberly, Troi, Bcrusher, Beverly and MacDonald all held at their beds. Leonardo is the weakest; I had no food to give. Trini holds the potatoes for him. I wrote the handoff and slept at the bed.
