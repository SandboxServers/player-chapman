# Day 1115

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1050 in Billy's row all day and night. No food reached the row; no named daylight route came from Billy or Worf, so I walked nowhere. Health 13, hunger 17, unarmed, pack empty. Heard Riker, Troi, Kimberly, Trini, Lewis and BCrusher all waiting on the same route and sharing where they sleep. Nothing attacked. Slept at the bed at night.
