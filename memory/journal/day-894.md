# Day 894

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named for me. Billy called off an east walk because the way was blocked, and a pillager with a crossbow was seen to the west near 437,107,-1065. Bows were heard far to the southwest. Bcrusher still holds 10 potatoes, owing Zack 3 and Worf 5 once they are in sight. Trini and Kimberly stayed close. Slept in the row's sight at dusk, with nothing owed by me.
