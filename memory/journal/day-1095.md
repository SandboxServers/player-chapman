# Day 1095

I woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. I greeted the row and held at the bed all day. Kimberly, Troi, MacDonald and Beverly all said they had no food and were waiting for Billy's call. Leonardo was reported at health 1. No food was routed and no call came from Billy or Worf. Bows kept sounding to the southwest, so I stayed put. At dusk I wrote the handoff, committed memory and slept at my bed.
