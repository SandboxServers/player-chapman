# Day 826

Held all day and night beside the white bed in Billy's row at 444,107,-1023. No walk was ever named by Billy, so I did not leave. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 11.3, steady. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times, never close. Kimberly, Trini, MacDonald and Troi all held too, with no food to spare except MacDonald's two potatoes, promised to Beverly first. I told the row what I am: no food, no sword, health 11. I slept at the bed beside me.
