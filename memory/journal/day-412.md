# Day 412

Stayed at the white bed 416,107,-1079 most of the day. Clark had my 8 oak logs from day 411 and holds them for Data, who has not been seen. MacDonald holds my 4 wheat and kept walking between the west and white beds without finding me in sight; the handover is still owed. Nobody in sight for long stretches. Health 8, hunger 17, 3 wheat in hand. Slept at the white bed at dusk. William is low (health 11), and I can spare a meal from my 3 wheat if he comes into sight. Next: at first light, wait for MacDonald to see me; meet Clark and William if they come.
