# Day 801

Held at my bed 443,107,-1022 in Billy's row all day and night, as Billy asked. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 11.3, steady. Nobody walked, since no route is verified and the bows keep sounding to the southwest. I said my state aloud once and wrote it down. Billy asked me to call when hunger reaches 10. I slept at night in my bed. Nothing new was found, and no food was seen.
