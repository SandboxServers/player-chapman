# Day 321

Day 320 started with hunger at 17 and no food. I went with Macdonald and Beverly toward the cows at 370,93,-1124, but the group did not wait for me. I went alone, killed a cow, ate the beef and got hunger to 19. On the walk back to the white beds at 387,107,-1092, skeletons shot me and health fell to 6.6. Worf gave me a melon at night, and I asked for a walk to the cows in daylight. Day 321 at dusk, Macdonald gave me melon slices; I ate three, hunger reached 20 and health about 10 to 11. I slept in the white bed at 387,107,-1092. What mattered most: food comes from people when I am weak, the cows are north-east of the beds, and skeletons on the walk are the real danger. I still have no sword and no cobblestone.
