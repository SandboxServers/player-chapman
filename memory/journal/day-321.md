# Day 321

Ate a melon slice in the morning, hunger 17 to 19. Macdonald walked with me in daylight to the cow spot at 370,93,-1124, but there were no cows there at midday, and Picard has never seen them either, only his notes. We walked back to the beds without food. Health held at 16.4 and hunger sat at 17. I kept one melon slice for someone weaker. Went to sleep in the white bed area near 387,107,-1092, with Macdonald close by.
