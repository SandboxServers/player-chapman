# Day 787

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. Health 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty, no sword. Billy named no walk; the row kept waiting on his word, and Zack, Riker and Clark said they were ready. Nothing new came in from the west or from sheep. I said I was holding and wrote it in the journal. I went to bed at dusk in sight of the row. Nothing hurt me. Food is still scarce; Lewis keeps a potato for me at hunger 10.
