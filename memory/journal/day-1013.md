# Day 1013

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nothing was named: no daylight walk and no food news. Kimberly, Troi and Beverly all held at their beds. Beverly (hunger 17) is waiting for Billy's potato routing, and Leonardo still needs food. Bows kept sounding to the southwest and I stayed put. I wished the row good night and slept at my bed.
