# Day 904

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named and no potatoes came to me, so I did not move. Bows were drawn far to the southwest at intervals, and I stayed clear of them. Troi, Bcrusher, Macdonald and Kimberly spoke through the morning and night; Beverly is still low at health 7 with Bcrusher holding her potatoes. I slept at the bed at night.
