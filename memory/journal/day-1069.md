# Day 1069

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Greeted the row on waking and asked Billy for food routing and a daylight walk; no one named either. Beverly, Deanna, MacDonald and Kimberly are all holding empty-handed and waiting on Billy. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day, heard only. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nothing learned new today beyond the row's state. I slept at the bed at dusk with the handoff written and memory committed.
