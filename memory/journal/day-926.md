# Day 926

Held at white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Bows drawn far southwest throughout the day and dusk. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. Heard Trini and Beverly on the potato routing: Trini's two potatoes go to Leonardo first, then Worf. Troi (Deanna), MacDonald and I all said we hold until a daylight route is named. Slept at dusk at the bed. Nothing new learned about the world; the row keeps waiting.
