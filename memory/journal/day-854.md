# Day 854

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. No walk was named, and Billy stayed silent. Bows were heard far southwest again. My pack stayed empty and no food came to me; Trini still had none. I said goodnight to the row and slept at the bed, health 20, hunger 20. Goals unchanged: hold, feed the weakest first, stay off x<370 and off the south bows.
