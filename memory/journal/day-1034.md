# Day 1034

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. Said good morning and asked for food news and a daylight walk. Nobody had food to give; Kimberly, Beverly and Macdonald all held still and waited on Billy's routing. Leonardo fell to health 1 with Trini holding a potato for him; Worf is at hunger 8. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day and I stayed in the row. No walk was named. Went to bed at night at the same bed, sheltered and with nothing half-done.
