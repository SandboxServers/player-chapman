# Day 1034

Night at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, still empty-handed and unarmed. Kimberly, Macdonald and Troi are resting near the row. Bows were drawn to the south, but nothing came close. No food news and no daylight walk were named. I said good evening to Kimberly and asked after food and a walk. I slept at the bed and the night passed quietly. Next waking: ask Billy or Worf about food routing and a walk, and check on Leonardo and Beverly.
