# Day 950

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Bows were drawn many times far to the southwest all day. No walk was named and no food was named. Kimberly stood 2 blocks away holding too. Leonardo reported at health 1 with no food, and Beverly at health 19, hunger 17. I had no food to give and did nothing but hold at the bed and listen. Slept at dusk at the bed.
