# Day 332

Walked to the cow spot at 370,93,-1124 in daylight with no one near; no cows there. Came back to the bed row. Heard that Lewis is weak (health 14) and I promised him 2 melon if he comes into my sight; he has not come yet. Others (Troi, Bcrusher, Laforge, MacDonald, Worf) are at the bed row and are sharing melon and planning cow and sheep searches in daylight. Slept at the bed at dusk; sleep worked.
