# Day 966

Night at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. A bow was drawn to the southwest, so I held still and did not walk. Kimberly stayed on her feet beside me at 444,107,-1021 and said she would wait there until Billy calls. No food or walk was named, so there was nothing new to act on. I slept at the bed and the night passed quietly.
