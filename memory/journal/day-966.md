# Day 966

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly beside me. Bows were drawn far to the southwest all day. No walk and no food was named by Billy or Worf. Beverly (hunger 17) and Leonardo (weakest) are still waiting on potato routing. I have no food to give, so I held at the bed, listened, and slept there at dusk.
