# Day 947

I woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. I greeted Kimberly, who stood two blocks off and was holding nothing. All day I held still. Bows were drawn far to the southwest again and again, and I stayed put. Kimberly and Troi said they were waiting on Billy to name a walk or bed. Beverly, at hunger 17, is waiting on Billy for potato routing. Leonardo is at health 1, and Troi has no food to give him. I have nothing to give anyone. Nothing new was named. I slept at the bed at dusk.
