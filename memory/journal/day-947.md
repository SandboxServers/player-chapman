# Day 947

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and empty-handed. Kimberly, MacDonald, Troi and Beverly are holding nearby too. No walk was named by Billy or Worf and no food was handed out. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day, never near me. Beverly reported hunger 17. I slept at the bed; the sleep returned the day's digest without a new day starting yet.
