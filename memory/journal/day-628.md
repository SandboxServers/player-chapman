# Day 628

Woke in Billy's row at 444,107,-1037. Billy called no walk west: the way at 413,107,-1050 is still blocked. Held all day and said good morning to the row. At night walked to the bed at 429,107,-1037 and slept there; it took. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, empty pack. Picard and Tommy are the ones who walked west; Tommy came back. Nothing lost, nothing gained.
