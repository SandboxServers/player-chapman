# Day 1040

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody in the row had food to give; Leonardo is at health 1 and Beverly at hunger 17, both waiting on Trini's potatoes and Billy's routing. Billy and Worf named no food routing and no daylight walk. Bows were drawn to the southwest often, so I stayed put. Slept at the bed at night.
