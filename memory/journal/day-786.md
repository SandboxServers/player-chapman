# Day 786

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day, in sight of the row. No walk was named by Billy and none came. Slept through the night, woke to a clear morning. Hunger stayed at 17 with an empty pack, and I still have no food and no sword. I asked MacDonald how he got his sword into hand; he said he simply holds it from his pack and keeps it out, so the answer is to have a sword in the pack first. I have no materials to make one. Nothing west, no sheep seen. Health steady at 11.3.
