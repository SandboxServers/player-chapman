# Day 1145

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, 443,107,-1051. No daylight route was ever named by Billy or Worf, so no food reached anyone. I told the row I am empty-handed too. Tommy, Kimberly, Beverly and Meriwether all spoke of waiting for the same route. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. I did not move. Slept at dusk in the bed; the row is still waiting.
