# Day 915

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Bows were drawn again and again to the southwest, never near us. Kimberly stood beside me. Leonardo is at health 1 and I have no food to give him. Troi, Macdonald and Bcrusher all said the same: nothing to share, waiting on Billy for a walk or a food routing. No walk was named. I slept at dusk at the bed.
