# Day 915

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row through the whole night and into the morning. Health and hunger stayed at 20; pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly stood beside me and the row answered each other's good evenings. Bows were drawn far off to the southwest several times, which kept us all in place. Billy named no walk and no potato routing, so Leonardo, Trini, Beverly and Bcrusher still wait on food. Trini has two potatoes ready for Leonardo once he walks to her sight. I had nothing to give. I slept at the bed at dusk on day 916.
