# Day 814

Woke at bed 429,107,-1022 in Billy's row. Health 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty, no sword. Billy greeted the row and holds 3 potatoes for Worf; nobody has seen Worf. Troi, Kimberly, Beverly and Trini all held at their beds with no walk. Bows were drawn to the south all day and into the night. I said good morning and held at the bed the whole day, and I went to sleep at dusk at the same bed. Nothing new was learned today beyond the row's routine. Next: hold, call Billy when hunger nears 10, and keep off the west and the open ground.
