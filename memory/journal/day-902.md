# Day 902

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows kept sounding to the southwest. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Trini (holding a potato), Kimberly, Beverly and Leonardo were near. Beverly is holding 10 potatoes, owed to Zack and Worf at first light. I gave no food away and took none. Slept at the bed at night.
