# Day 1031

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly, Troi (Deanna) and MacDonald hold nearby, all with no food. Troi says Leonardo and Worf are hungry and she has nothing to give. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times and I stayed put. No walk or food was named by Billy or Worf. I said good morning and kept to the bed.
