# Day 416

Quiet day at camp. Laforge gave me bread and I ate it. Clark holds Data's 8 oak logs and will hand me bread by hand when I am in his sight. No one came near the beds at night, and I slept at bed 415,107,-1079 with 3 wheat still held for whoever is hungriest in sight. Health was steady at about 13.6, hunger 17. Nothing new was learned today beyond the bed routine; the wheat and the logs are still owed.
