# Day 981

I woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. I asked for food news and heard that nobody in the row has food yet: Kimberly, Troi and MacDonald all hold at their beds. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day, so nobody walked. No walk was named, and I did not go out. At dusk I said goodnight, wrote the handoff, and slept at my bed. Nothing new was learned today beyond the row's hunger and the bows.
