Day 981. Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and night, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times and I stayed put. No walk and no food was named. Leonardo is at health 1 at bed 416 with Troi beside him; Trini holds the potatoes for him. Beverly is at 19 health, 17 hunger, waiting on Billy's potato routing. I told the row I have nothing to give and that I stay here. Slept at the bed.
