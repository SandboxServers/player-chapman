# Day 378

Stayed at the white bed near 388,107,-1079 all day with the group. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Data never came into sight, so the 8 oak logs are still in my pack. Billy was reported east near 443, out of my sight; no cow walk was called. Kimberly was reported short of food and Tommy had melon; my one melon slice stayed in reserve. Slept at night beside Zack and Riker; days_since_sleep is 0. Nothing new was learned beyond the camp's waiting. Next: keep waiting for Data, hand over the logs, tell Clark.
