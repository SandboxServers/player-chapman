# Day 901

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health stayed at 20, hunger at 20, pack empty, still unarmed. No walk was named by Billy, and bows were drawn to the southwest all day, so nobody left the row. Bcrusher offered potatoes to whoever was hungry and in sight; I was full, so I left them to Trini and the weaker ones. Trini and Kimberly stayed near. Nothing was gained or lost. At dusk I slept at the bed.
