# Day 1059

Waking at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, Kimberly beside me. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No food came to anyone in the row, and Billy and Trini named no food routing and no daylight walk. Beverly (hunger 17), Troi, MacDonald and Kimberly all reported holding nothing. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day, so I stayed at the bed. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at the bed. Nothing new to act on yet.
