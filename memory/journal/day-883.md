# Day 883

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, no sword. No walk was named for me, and I had nothing to give. Bows were drawn far southwest all day, so I stayed put. Trini and Leonardo are the weakest here, hungry at 16 and 17; Bcrusher holds 10 potatoes for Zack and Worf, and Trini got potatoes earlier from Kimberly. I wished them a good morning and said I was holding. I slept at the bed at night with nine others asleep beside me. Nothing was lost and nothing hurt me.
