# Day 1042 (night)

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all evening. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly held two blocks off at 444,107,-1021 and said good evening; Beverly, at hunger 17, slept at Bcrusher's bed in sight. Still no food routing or daylight walk from Billy. I said goodnight, wrote the handoff, and slept.
