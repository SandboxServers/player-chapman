# Day 1042

I woke at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack. I said good morning and asked Billy and Worf for food routing and a daylight walk. Neither answered all day. The row held still: Deanna, Beverly (hunger 17), Kimberly, and MacDonald all at their beds with no food. Leonardo is at health 1 and Trini holds the potatoes for him first. Bows were heard to the southwest throughout. I held at my bed, said goodnight to the row, and slept.
