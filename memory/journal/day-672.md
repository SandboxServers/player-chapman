# Day 672

Held at my bed at 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day, in his sight. No walk was named: Billy has no verified route yet, so everyone holds. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food to pass. Heard the row's morning and dusk greetings; Worf, Riker, Picard, Trini and others also hold. MacDonald (health 11) and Trini (health 17) are thin. Went to bed at dusk and slept in sight of the row.
