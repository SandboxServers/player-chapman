# Day 795

Held at the bed in Billy's row all day, hunger 17, health 11.3, pack empty. Billy named no walk: still no verified route west and no food or sheep seen. Zack, Clark, Kimberly and Billy all held in sight. Bows kept sounding to the southwest. At night I went to the bed at 443,107,-1022 and slept (6 of 10 asleep). Nothing new learned; the row is holding steady.
