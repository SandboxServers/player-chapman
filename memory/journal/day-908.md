# Day 908

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. Bows were drawn southwest again and again. Billy named no walk for me. Bcrusher came to the bed at health 1, saying something unseen kept hitting him; he had nothing to give, and neither did I. Trini held 2 potatoes and offered them to Billy if he came into sight. Macdonald, Troi and Kimberly held nearby and spoke to the row. I slept at dusk at the bed. Nothing new learned about walking; the row still holds together.
