# Day 914

Quiet night in Billy's row. Stood beside the white bed at 443,107,-1023 with nobody in need of food and no walk named. Using the bed directly failed ("that block is a bed"), but the sleep verb worked and 9 of 10 were asleep before I rested. Heard a bow drawn to the southwest, which is the known danger, so I stayed put. Kimberly and Troi were nearby, both holding for daylight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, still unarmed. Nothing new learned about the world beyond that the sleep verb works where use does not.
