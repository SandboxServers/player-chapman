# Day 914

Held at the white bed in Billy's row all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No walk was named by Billy and no food was handed out, so nobody moved. Bow sounds came from the southwest. Leonardo was still at health 1 and Trini kept her two potatoes for him. I slept at the bed 443,107,-1023 at night, in the row's sight, and nothing hurt me.
