# Day 914

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Held at the bed all day as the row asked. No walk was named and no food was handed out. Bows were drawn far to the southwest several times, so I did not walk. Kimberly, Bcrusher, Troi and Macdonald all held too. Night came and I slept at my bed.
