# Day 755

Held at my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day, in sight of the others. Billy called no walk: no verified route west, and nobody has seen one. Picard, Trini, Lewis, Laforge and Billy all report health 20 or 17 and hunger 20 or 17; nobody needs food yet. Laforge holds no food, only a wooden sword. The promised sword has not come. I slept at night at my bed. Nothing new was learned today except that the row is steady and still waiting.
