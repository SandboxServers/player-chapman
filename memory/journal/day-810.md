# Day 810

Woke at bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 11.3, hunger 17, no food, no sword. Nobody called a walk all day. Billy, Trini, Troi, Kimberly and MacDonald all held at their beds. Bows kept sounding to the south. I said good morning, held, and at dusk went to my bed 429,107,-1022 and slept. Hunger still 17, above the call line of 10.
