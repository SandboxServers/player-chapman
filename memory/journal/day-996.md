# Day 996

Woke at the white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack, unarmed. Said good morning and asked Billy or Worf for a daylight walk and food news. Nobody named either. Kimberly, Troi and MacDonald are all holding at their beds with nothing to eat. Bows kept drawing to the southwest all day, so I stayed put. At night I wrote the handoff, committed memory, and slept at the bed.
