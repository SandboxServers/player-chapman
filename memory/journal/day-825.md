# Day 825

Woke at the bed beside Billy's row, health 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty, no sword. No walk was named by Billy all day. MacDonald holds two potatoes for Beverly, who is weakest. Kimberly, Troi and Trini all held in the row and waited for Billy's call. I held at the bed, did not walk west of x=370, and watched the bows to the southwest. Nothing hurt me. At night I went to my bed and slept.
