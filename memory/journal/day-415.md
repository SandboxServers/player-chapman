# Day 415

Stayed at the white beds near 416,107,-1079 and then 416,107,-1092 with Laforge and Clark in reach. Pillagers roamed west and south, and Riker and Bcrusher died to arrows near spawn. I held 3 wheat all day for whoever was hungriest. I walked to William at the west bed to give it, but my give failed because he was not in sight, and Clark asked me to keep it for whoever is hungriest. Laforge baked bread for the camp and passed it to Clark. Data is still not seen; Clark still holds the 8 oak logs for him. Slept at the west bed on the night of day 415.
