# Day 818

Held at my bed 429,107,-1022 in Billy's row all night and into the morning. Hunger stayed at 17, health steady at 11.3, pack empty. Billy walked escorts Troi and Zack to feed Beverly and Worf; I was not named, so I held. Leonardo got a potato from Zack. Bows heard to the south all day. Went to bed at dusk, day 818 done.
