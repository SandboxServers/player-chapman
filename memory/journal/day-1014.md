# Day 1014

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, night with thunder. Greeted the row and held. Kimberly, MacDonald, Troi and Beverly all hold at their beds with empty packs; Leonardo is still at health 1 and Beverly at hunger 17. No walk was named and no food reached anyone. A bow was drawn far to the southwest. I slept at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty and unarmed. Nothing was lost today.
