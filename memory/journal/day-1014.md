# Day 1014

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, unarmed and empty-handed. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Bows were drawn far to the southwest again and again; I stayed put. Kimberly, MacDonald and Troi are holding at their beds too. Kimberly's sword is lost at 421,107,-1047, and Leonardo is still at health 1 while Trini's potatoes go to him first. No walk and no food was named by Billy or Worf. I slept at the bed at night and woke to the same waiting.
