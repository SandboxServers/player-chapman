# Day 891

Quiet day at the white bed in Billy's row at 443,107,-1023. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, still unarmed. No walk was named, so I held. Bows were heard far to the southwest, and I stayed away from them. Billy, Kimberly and Trini were near, and Billy spoke clearly about staying put until daylight. I slept in the bed; 7 of 10 needed were asleep. Nothing new was learned beyond the row's routine, and no one was given food today. Next: at first light, hold unless Billy names a daylight walk with an armed friend in sight.
