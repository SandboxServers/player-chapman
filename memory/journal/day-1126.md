# Day 1126

Dusk, clear. Stood at the white bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row with nobody near. Health steady at 13, hunger 17, pack empty, still unarmed and without food. No named daylight route came from Billy or Worf. I rested the night in the bed. Nothing new to act on; food and the potatoes at 463-470 remain the open questions.
