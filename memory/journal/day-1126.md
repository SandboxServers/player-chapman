# Day 1126

Held at my white bed 444,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Still no food reached the row and no daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, so nobody walked. Trini, Kimberly, Beverly and Riker all held at their beds and said they would feed Leonardo first when food comes. Said my status aloud at dawn. Dusk came; I wrote the handoff and slept at the bed.
