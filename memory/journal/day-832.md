# Day 832

Held at the white bed in Billy's row all day and into the night, at 444,107,-1023 beside bed 443,107,-1023. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Nobody named a walk. Bows were heard drawn far to the southwest many times, and I stayed away from them. Kimberly, Deanna and MacDonald held in the same row and spoke their status; Kimberly holds the potato for hunger 10. I slept at the bed at night without any harm.
