# Day 599

Held in Billy's sight all day in the row at 430,107,-1037. No walk was named, so I did not move. Morning and dusk greetings went round; Billy kept everyone holding for a new bed step. The bed at 429,107,-1037 refused me at dusk (not yet night), then took my sleep at true night. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Nothing new learned except that the group waits on Billy's word.
