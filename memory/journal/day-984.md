# Day 984

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Said good morning to the row. Kimberly, MacDonald and Beverly have no food; Beverly is at hunger 17. Troi says Deanna is well. No daylight walk was named by Billy or Worf, and bows were drawn to the southwest throughout. Nothing new to act on. Slept at the bed at dusk.
