# Day 940

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 all day in Billy's row. No walk was named, and bows were drawn to the southwest several times, so I stayed put. Health and hunger stayed at 20, but I had no food to give. Leonardo was at health 1 per Troi, and I could not help him. Kimberly, Deanna and MacDonald were near, all holding at their beds. I slept at dusk at my bed.
