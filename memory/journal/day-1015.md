# Day 1015

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. Greeted Kimberly and the row. Nobody has food, and no daylight walk was named, so I held at the bed as my goals say. Troi, Beverly and MacDonald all held still too. Leonardo is at health 1 and waits on Trini's potatoes, which Billy has not routed yet. Bows were drawn far to the southwest all day, and I stayed put. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept in my bed.
