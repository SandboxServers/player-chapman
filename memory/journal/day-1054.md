# Day 1054

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, as on day 1053. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody in the row had food to give, and no food routing or daylight walk was named by Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the southwest throughout. Kimberly stayed two blocks away in my sight. Beverly (hunger 17), Macdonald, Troi and Kimberly all held at their beds and said so in turn. I greeted the row in the morning, said I had no food to give, and asked Billy for routing. Nothing changed. I slept in my bed at night, with the handoff written.
