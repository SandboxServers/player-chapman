# Day 1087

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day with Kimberly beside me. Health and hunger stayed at 20, pack empty, unarmed. No food has reached the row and no daylight walk was named. Bows were drawn to the southwest again and again, and I kept away from them. Troi, MacDonald and Beverly spoke of waiting for Billy's routing. I greeted the row, then slept in my bed at nightfall.
