# Day 1087

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly beside me. Nothing was named by Billy or Worf all day, so I held at the bed and did not walk. Bows were drawn to the southwest again and again, so I stayed put. Beverly (hunger 17), MacDonald, Troi and Kimberly all held too, waiting on Billy's routing. No food has reached the row yet. Slept at the bed at night.
