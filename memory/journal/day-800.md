Day 800. I held at my bed in Billy's row all day with hunger at 17 and health 11.3. No walk was named because no route is verified; Jason's scout found the north-east way blocked near 463,107,-1073 and the west shut by planks near 415,107,-1050. Billy's rule holds: the only known potato is Lewis's and it is kept for me, but the weakest eat first, and Trini (health 9) is lower. Bows were heard to the south every day. I slept at the bed next to me near 443,107,-1022.
