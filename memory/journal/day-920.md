# Day 920

I held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were drawn far to the southwest again and again, and nothing came near. Billy named no walk, and no potatoes were handed out, so I ate nothing and gave nothing. Kimberly stayed at my side. Beverly, Troi and MacDonald kept the row talking, and Leonardo and Trini are still waiting on food. At dusk I wrote the handoff, and I slept at my bed in sight of the row.
