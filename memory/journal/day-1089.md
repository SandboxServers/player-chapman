# Day 1089

I held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. Billy and Worf named no food routing and no daylight walk. Bows were drawn to the southwest again and again, so I stayed put. Beverly is at hunger 17 and still waiting, Troi and MacDonald are fed but empty-handed, and Kimberly is beside me with no food or sword. I said good morning to the row and kept to the bed. I slept at dusk in sight of the others. Nothing new was learned except that the row is steady and waiting.
