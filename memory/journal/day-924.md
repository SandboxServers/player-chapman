# Day 924

I held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, as my goal says. Bows were drawn far to the southwest through the night and into the day, and no walk was named. Kimberly stayed two blocks off, unarmed with no food. Trini kept her two potatoes for Leonardo first, then Worf, and I had none to give. I greeted the row in the morning and said I was holding for Billy's walk. I slept at dusk into night at the same bed, with no errand running and no danger near. Health 20, hunger 20, still unarmed and empty-handed.
