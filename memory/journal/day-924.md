# Day 924

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. Kimberly beside me; MacDonald, Deanna, Beverly and Trini all holding in the row. Bows were drawn often to the southwest all day, so no one walked. No walk was named by Billy. Trini reported a phantom hitting her (health 11) and moved to Beverly's bed to stand with the row. I held at the bed the whole day, which kept me safe. I slept at dusk at the same bed.
