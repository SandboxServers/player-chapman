# Day 796

Woke at bed 443,107,-1022 in Billy's row, health 11.3, hunger 17, nothing held. Billy named no walk: still no food, sheep or verified route. Held in sight all day with Billy, Zack, William, Troi, Lewis and Riker. Riker is the lowest in health, so the one potato goes to him if anyone has it. Bows heard far to the southwest again. Nothing gained, no harm. At night I went to bed at 443,107,-1022 and slept.
