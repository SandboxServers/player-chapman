# Day 974

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Bows kept being drawn far to the southwest. Deanna, MacDonald, Beverly and Kimberly all held their beds and spoke in turn; nobody named a food route or a daylight walk. I told Deanna I am holding and asked about food and walks. Beverly (hunger 17) is still the weakest and still waiting for Billy's routing. I had nothing to give. Slept at the bed.
