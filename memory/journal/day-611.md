# Day 611

Held in Billy's sight all day at 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and empty-handed. Billy named no walk for me; he let Jason walk to his bed in daylight. At dusk the beds in the row were tried, and at true night my sleep at bed 429,107,-1037 took 5 of 10, then the second sleep finished it. Nothing else happened to me today.
