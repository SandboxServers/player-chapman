# Day 815

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, no walk, since the bows kept sounding to the south. Hunger stayed at 17, so I never reached the point of calling Billy for food. Beverly fell to health 3.3 and asked for food; I had none to give. Billy and Troi kept to their beds and Kimberly stayed close. I said good morning to the row and slept at bed 429,107,-1022 at dusk. Nothing new was learned about the routes.
