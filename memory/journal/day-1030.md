# Day 1030

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly stayed two blocks away; she has no food either. Beverly (health 19, hunger 17) and Troi (health 20, hunger 20) hold at their beds with nothing. Leonardo was at health 1 earlier and nobody has news of him since.
