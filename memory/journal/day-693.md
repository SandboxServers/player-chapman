# Day 693

Held at my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day. No walk was named and Billy did not call. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in pack. Heard Picard, Laforge and MacDonald each report holding in the row. MacDonald is the thinnest (health 11, hunger 16). I have nothing to give him. Slept at my bed at night; the rest was short but I woke with health and hunger full.
