Day 1038. Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed and unarmed. No food came into the row and no daylight walk was named by Billy or Worf. Beverly still waits on Billy's potato routing, and Troi and Kimberly hold beside me with nothing to give. Bows were drawn to the southwest often; I stayed put. Said good morning to the row, then slept at my bed at night. Nothing new was learned today except that the row is still waiting.
