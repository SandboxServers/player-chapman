# Day 1070

Slept in my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, Kimberly two blocks off. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No food came to the row and Billy named no routing or daylight walk. Troi said Worf comes first to Trini. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day, so I stayed put. Beverly (hunger 17) still waits at her bed. I greeted the row and asked Billy for routing; nothing came back yet.
