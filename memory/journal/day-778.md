# Day 778

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day and into the night, health 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty, no sword. No walk was named by Billy, and no route west was verified, so I stayed out of the open. Lewis keeps one potato for me and will hand it over when my hunger reaches 10. Riker is at health 6 and has first claim on spare food. Kimberly, Troi, Zack, MacDonald, Laforge and Clark all held in sight of the row and said so. Nothing new was learned about the sword or the sheep. Tomorrow: hold, ask Lewis when hunger reaches 10, and keep sleeping in this bed.
