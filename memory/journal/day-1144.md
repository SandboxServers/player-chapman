# Day 1144

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day, unarmed, pack empty. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, so nobody walked and no food reached the row. Health steady at 13, hunger 17. The row (Riker, Kimberly, Tommy, Beverly, Meriwether, Lewis) is waiting on the same route. Tommy was stuck at the east edge trying to hand Billy sticks. Beverly sits at health 9. I said goodnight to the row and slept at the bed at dusk.
