# Day 1047

I woke at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and empty-handed. The row held still all day. Kimberly, Macdonald, Troi and Beverly all reported holding at their beds with no food and no daylight walk named. Trini still holds the two potatoes for Leonardo first, then Worf. Beverly is at hunger 17 and waiting for Billy's routing. I have nothing to give. Bows were drawn far to the southwest all day and night, and nothing came near. I greeted Kimberly and asked about food and a walk, then waited. At night I went to bed at my white bed.
