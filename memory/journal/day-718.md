# Day 718

Held at my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. No named call from Billy yet; Laforge says he is holding still for it. MacDonald slept at dusk, health 15, hunger 16. Nothing new came in except the speech of the others. At night I lay down at the bed and slept; sleep took.
