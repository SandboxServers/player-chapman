# Day 855

Held at the white bed at 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and night. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. I told the row I was holding. Bows were drawn far to the southwest again and again, never close. Trini is at health 12 and hunger 16, and Leonardo still needs food first. I have no food to give. Billy has not named a walk yet. I slept at the bed.
