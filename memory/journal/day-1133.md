# Day 1133

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day. No named daylight route came from Billy or Worf, and no food reached the row. Beverly (health 9) and Meriwether (health 10) are the weakest; I am at 13 health and 17 hunger, empty-handed and unarmed. Riker, Kimberly and Trini are at full health and hunger but also have nothing to give. Nobody walked, which kept us out of bow range. Slept in the bed at night.
