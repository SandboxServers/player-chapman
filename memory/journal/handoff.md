# Handoff

- Standing at 444,107,-1051 beside the white bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row. Not enclosed. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed.
- Dusk on day 1145. Sleep call returned a digest only; no rest happened. Bed use refused. Pillagers have been to the south and west of the row.
