# Handoff (day 1093, dusk)

- At white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly beside me.
- No errand running, not in danger. Going to sleep here.
- Still no food routed to the row; Billy silent. Bows keep sounding southwest; do not go southwest or west of x=370.
- On waking: greet the row, hold at the bed until Billy or Worf names a call. Beverly (hunger 17) and Worf are the first to feed.
