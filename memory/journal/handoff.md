# Handoff (dusk day 526)

- At 405,107,-1036, at the row, 1.4 blocks from Billy, in sight of Picard and Lewis. Health 20, hunger 20.
- Holding my stone_sword in hand. No food. Not enclosed.
- Days since sleep 32. Beds have refused me at night.
- Billy was shot at on the west oak walk (day 526); the walk is held. Billy at health 8, Laforge and Trini gave bread.
- Trini died at the row night 524. Tommy, Leonardo, Laforge gave wooden swords to Billy.
- Next: hold still in Billy's sight through the night. Walk only on Billy's named call in daylight, with four armed in his sight.
