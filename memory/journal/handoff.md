# Handoff (night, day 953)

- At white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Kimberly near.
- Nothing new all day except bows drawn far to the southwest. Troi asked if anyone is hungry or hurt; I answered I have no food.
- Next waking: hold at the bed. If Billy or Worf names a daylight walk with armed friends, or food is named, go to Leonardo first.
