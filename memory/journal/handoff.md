# Handoff (night, day 941)

- At white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty.
- Holding all day again. No food named or handed to me, no walk named. Bows heard to the southwest often; stayed put.
- Beverly holds at hunger 17; Leonardo was weakest. I still have no food to give.
