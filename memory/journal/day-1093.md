# Day 1093

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, with Kimberly beside me. Health and hunger stayed at 20 and the pack stayed empty. No food reached the row and Billy stayed silent, so nobody moved. Bows kept sounding to the southwest the whole time; I did not go that way. I greeted the row at waking and told them I was still holding. Beverly, at hunger 17, is still the first to feed when food comes. I slept at dusk at the bed, with the handoff and memory committed first.
