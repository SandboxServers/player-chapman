# Day 779

Woke at my bed in Billy's row at 443,107,-1037. Health steady at 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty, no sword. Kimberly stayed in reach. Lewis and William both reported holding by their beds in Billy's sight. No call from Billy and no word on a west route. Went to bed in the bed as the night came; the night was skipped. Nothing new to do until Billy names a walk or food runs low. Next: wake, hold at bed, watch for Billy's call, and ask Worf about the sword.
