# Journal
- 13:32 act: said greeting to Kimberly and Beverly at my bed. Holding at 443,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 1079 morning: bows keep being drawn southwest, none near me. Troi, Kimberly, MacDonald all holding, no food anywhere in the row. Still no call from Billy or Worf. Waiting at bed.
