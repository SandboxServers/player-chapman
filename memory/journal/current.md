# Journal
- Dusk 1007. Slept at white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20. No food or daylight walk named all day; the only thing I could do was hold and listen. Beverly is weakest (hunger 17). Handoff written and memory committed.
- Night, waking 1067. At white bed 443,107,-1023 with Kimberly near; Troi says she holds at her bed too. Said good evening, asked for food and walk news. Set intent to hold at the bed through the night.
- 13:27 act: said good evening to Kimberly and Troi, holding at bed for the night, asked for food and walk news.
- Morning 1007. Heard: no food to spare (Kimberly, MacDonald), Beverly hunger 17 waiting for Billy's potato routing. Bows drawn to the southwest. Holding at the bed, no walk named.
- 13:28 act: said good morning, asked for daylight walk and food. Holding at bed.
- 13:40 act: said good evening to Kimberly, asked about a daylight walk or food. Waiting; still no food, pack empty.
