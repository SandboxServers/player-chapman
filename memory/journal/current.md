# Journal
- Day 1030 morning. At my white bed 443,107,-1023. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Kimberly, Troi, Beverly hold with no food. Said good morning and that I will hold until Billy or Worf names a walk. No walk named; bows drawn southwest.
- 21:12 act: said good morning to Kimberly, asked for food news and a daylight walk. Kimberly is 2 blocks away. Holding at bed.
- Wait: bows drawn southwest twice more. No food, no walk named yet.
