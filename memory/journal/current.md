# Journal
- Day 810 morning, at bed 429,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty. Said good morning. Held at bed; bow sounds to the south all day, no walk called.
- Day 811 dusk, still at bed in Billy's row. Health 11.3, hunger 17. Trini greeted me, offered a potato if I am weakest; I am not yet at 10. Going to sleep in my bed for the night.
