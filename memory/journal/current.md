# Journal
- 11:21 act: said greeting at white bed 443,107,-1023; asked Billy for food routing or a daylight walk. Holding. Kimberly two blocks off. No food known. Day 1073 now. Bows heard southwest; Leonardo at health 1. I have nothing to give; holding at bed for Billy's named call. Billy still silent; no food arrived. Waiting. Dusk now; Deanna holding nearby too. Plan: sleep at this bed when night comes. Row is settling for the night; I still hold, no food, no walk named. Morning again; row is holding at beds, all waiting on Billy. Still holding. Day 1074; days since sleep is 2, so I should sleep at this bed at the next night.
