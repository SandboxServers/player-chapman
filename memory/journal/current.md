# Journal
- Night, day 1069. At white bed 443,107,-1023 with Kimberly two blocks off. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Heard a bow drawn to the southwest. Greeted Kimberly and asked about food.
- Morning. Kimberly, Deanna and Beverly all hold at their beds, no food yet, waiting for Billy's named route. I am holding at my bed and will not walk until Billy names a daylight walk.
