# Journal
- Waking 1099, night, day 1025 still. At bed 443,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Kimberly two blocks off, still no food and no walk named. A bow was drawn to the southwest. Said I would hold at the bed till morning and wait for Billy's word.
- Day 1026 morning: said good morning to the row. Deanna, Beverly and MacDonald are at their beds with no food; Beverly at hunger 17. No walk or food named yet. Still holding at my bed, waiting for Billy or Worf.
