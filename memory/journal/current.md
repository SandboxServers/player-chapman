# Journal
- Dusk, day 1052 (waking 1156). At my white bed 443,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, no food, unarmed. Sleep refused: not night yet. Greeted Kimberly; she has no word from Billy and will not walk at dusk. Bows drawn to the southwest again. Macdonald and Beverly (hunger 17) still waiting for Billy's routing. Morning now; still holding at the bed, no walk named.
