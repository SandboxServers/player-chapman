# Day 780

Woke at 443,107,-1021 in the open, walked back to my bed at -1037 and held there all day with the row. No walk was named by Billy, and nobody has called. Health steady at 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty, still no sword. Heard a bow drawn to the southwest. Asked MacDonald how he got his sword into hand: he said he took it from the pack "with use", which I did not fully understand. Lewis does not know either. Slept at dusk at my bed, row in sight.
