# Day 1056

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nothing to give: I have no food. Leonardo fell to health 1 and Worf and Beverly are short of food. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo. Billy never named a food routing or a daylight walk. Bows were drawn to the southwest the whole time; I never went near them. Said good morning to Kimberly, Troi, Beverly and MacDonald, and asked Billy for routing. Nothing else changed.
