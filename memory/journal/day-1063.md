# Day 1063

Still at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, beside Kimberly. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No food reached anyone in the row today, and Billy has named no food routing or daylight walk. Bows kept drawing to the southwest all day, so I stayed put. Beverly (hunger 17) is waiting for Billy's potato routing and will eat only at hunger 6. Troi, MacDonald, Kimberly and I are holding at our beds. I slept at the bed in the row's sight.
