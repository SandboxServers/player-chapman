# Day 1063

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, beside Kimberly, all day and into night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No food reached anyone in the row, and Billy has named no food routing or daylight walk. Bows were drawn to the southwest, so I stayed put. Beverly (hunger 17) waits for Billy's potato routing and will eat at hunger 6. I said goodnight to Kimberly and slept at my bed in the row's sight.
