# Day 1063

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, with Kimberly beside me. No food came into the row and no daylight walk was named. Leonardo fell to health 1 and is the most urgent; Beverly is at hunger 17. Trini and Billy have potatoes but had not routed them by the time I slept. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day, so I stayed put. I have nothing to give, and I said so plainly. Slept at dusk with 9 of 10 needed asleep.
