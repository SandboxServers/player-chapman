# Day 598

Held in the row at 430,107,-1023 all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, empty pack. Billy named no walk except Trini's short try to reach her bed 429,107,-1037; the bed refused her in daylight, and Billy ordered everyone to hold until night. Bows were drawn to the south much of the day, but nothing came into the row. At dusk Billy slept at 443,107,-1037 and I stayed put, planning to sleep here in sight of the group.
