# Day 948

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No daylight walk or food was named. Bows were drawn to the southwest again and again, but nothing came near. Troi, Kimberly, MacDonald and Beverly all held at their beds and kept listening for Leonardo, Trini and Riker. I said I was holding. Slept at the bed at night; nothing changed in the sleep.
