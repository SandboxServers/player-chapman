# Day 387

Woke at the white beds near 388,107,-1079 with health and hunger at 20. Waited most of the day for Data, who has not yet come into sight, so the 8 oak logs are still with me. Zack came to the bed row and is waiting for Billy's call; he offered food if anyone is low. MacDonald went to the shore to plant. Nobody was hurt. I slept at the bed at night, so days_since_sleep is 0 again.
