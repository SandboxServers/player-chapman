# Day 1078

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody has food to give and Billy has named no food routing or daylight walk. Leonardo fell to health 1 with Trini holding the potatoes. Bows were heard drawn to the southwest all day, so I did not move. I greeted the row in the morning and asked Billy directly for routing; no answer came. Went to bed at dusk at my bed. Nothing new learned except that the row is steady but waiting.
