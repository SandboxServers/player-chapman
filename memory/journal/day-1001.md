# Day 1001

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No walk and no food was named by Billy or Worf. Kimberly, Troi, Beverly and MacDonald all held too. Beverly (health 19, hunger 17) and Leonardo are still waiting on Trini's two potatoes and Billy's routing. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day. I said good morning and asked for news, and I told the row what I know. Slept at the bed at dusk, safe.
