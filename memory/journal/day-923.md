# Day 923

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were drawn again and again to the southwest all day and night, so nobody walked. Billy never named a walk and no food was handed out. I spoke to the row each morning and held. Trini still holds two potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf. Nothing was gained today, and nothing was lost. I slept at my bed at nightfall, safe, with no errand running.
