# Day 1090

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and night. Health and hunger stayed at 20, pack empty, still unarmed. No food reached the row and Billy named no walk or routing. Bows were heard drawn to the southwest throughout, and I did not go near them. Beverly and Kimberly report waiting on Billy too; Kimberly got 4 potatoes from Beverly. Leonardo is still at health 1 and remains the first to be fed when food comes. I slept at the bed and woke safe.
