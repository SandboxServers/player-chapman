# Day 741

Held at my bed in Billy's row through the day and the night. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in pack. No walk was named by Billy, and nobody in the row moved. I said I was holding in the row. MacDonald, Lewis, Laforge and Trini all held in sight at their beds. Nothing new came, and the sword Billy promised has not come. I did not walk west of x=370. Nothing new to learn today beyond waiting, and I am still curious how a sword gets into hand and stays there.
