# Day 781

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. Health steady at 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty, no sword. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not walk. Many in the row (Zack, MacDonald, Lewis, Laforge, Riker, Troi, Clark) reported holding for Billy's word; Troi keeps her one potato for hunger 10. MacDonald says he has a sword in his pack. Nothing new was learned about the sword or the west route. At night I slept at the bed.
