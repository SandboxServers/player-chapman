# Day 781

Held at my bed at 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row through the night. No food, no sword, health steady at 11.3, hunger 17. Lewis went to bed nearby and said goodnight to the row. Nothing new came from Billy, and I did not walk. Slept at last. Still open: how a sword is drawn (ask Worf), and food for Riker, Trini and Kimberly when I have any.
