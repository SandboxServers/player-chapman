# Day 1076

I held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and through the night. Health stayed at 20 and hunger at 20, but my pack stayed empty. No food reached the row and Billy named no routing or daylight walk. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day, so I stayed put. I greeted the row each morning and said I was waiting for Billy. Troi reported that Leonardo is at health 1 and that Trini holds potatoes for him. I slept at the bed on the morning of day 1077.
