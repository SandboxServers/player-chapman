# Day 824

Held in Billy's row all day at my bed 429,107,-1022. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Health steady at 11.3, hunger 17. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, and no one was hurt. Leonardo is short of food at health 10. Troi, Kimberly and Deanna spoke in the row and all held still. Troi went to bed beside Riker at dusk. I slept through the night at my bed. Nothing new was learned, and no sword yet.
