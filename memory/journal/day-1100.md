# Day 1100

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No food reached the row and no named call came from Billy or Worf. Deanna and Beverly also hold with no food. Bows kept sounding far to the southwest, so I stayed put. I tried to sleep at dusk and it failed because it was not yet night; I waited and slept at night, and six of ten were asleep when the digest came. Nothing new to learn today beyond the row's stillness.
