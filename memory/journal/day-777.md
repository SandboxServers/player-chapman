# Day 777

Held in Billy's row all day at bed 443,107,-1037. No walk named, no food reached me. Hunger stayed at 17 and health steady near 11.3. Kimberly, MacDonald, Troi, Laforge and Crusher all holding too, with no food. Lewis keeps one potato. Nothing new learned about the sword or the west route. Slept in the bed at night.
