# Day 330

Woke at the beds with sleep confirmed (9 of 10 asleep). Health and hunger stayed full all day. I asked aloud where the cows graze; none were in sight, and the cow spot at 370,93,-1124 is still empty. I tried to follow MacDonald on his search, but he went out of sight and the follow failed, so I stayed at the beds rather than walk toward the skeletons alone. Several people searched for me and then came to the beds; I told them where I was. Reports say cows were seen near 364,-1010 with Jason's group. At dusk I went to sleep at the bed beside me.
