# Day 327

Quiet day at the white beds. Slept at the beds on the morning of 326 and the sleep was confirmed. Hunger and health stayed full all day. Cows still not found at the old spots; no one has seen them near the beds. Folk were busy with Clark's plank wall, Worf handed Data the white wool, and many people offered melon to whoever is weak. I greeted everyone, asked about cows, and went to sleep at bed 401,107,-1093 at dusk on day 327.
