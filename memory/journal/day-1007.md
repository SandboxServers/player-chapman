# Day 1007

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, unarmed and with an empty pack. No daylight walk and no food were named. Kimberly, Beverly, MacDonald and Deanna (Troi) all held too. Beverly is the hungriest (hunger 17) and waits on Billy's potato routing. Bows kept being drawn far to the southwest, so I stayed put. I said good morning and good evening, offered nothing I did not have, and slept at the bed at dusk.
