Day 318. Ate a melon slice and gave Picard two more, so Picard was fed and I kept one. Scouted west to x350 for animals: found only jungle logs and a drop, no animals, and I fell once and was hurt by something unseen on the way back. Back at the white beds, nobody was weak in sight, so my last melon is still held. Slept in the bed at 387,107,-1092 at night. Still no meat; animals remain the open question. Next: find out what hurt me, scout another direction, and tell Beverly and Kimberly what I found.
