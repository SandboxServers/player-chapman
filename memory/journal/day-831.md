# Day 831

I held at my bed in Billy's row all day and night. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Bows were drawn far to the southwest again and again, and I stayed out of their reach. Health and hunger stayed at 20, but my pack is empty and I have no food. I said my position aloud once and waited for Billy's word. Kimberly and Trini are holding food for whoever is weakest, and Trini is the one most in need. I slept beside my bed at night.
