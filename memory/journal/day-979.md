# Day 979

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and into night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk and no food were named. Beverly and Leonardo are still short of food; Trini holds potatoes for Leonardo. Kimberly, Deanna and MacDonald stayed at their beds in sight of the row. Bows were heard to the south. Slept in the white bed at night. Nothing new learned except that the row is holding steady and waiting for Billy's call.
