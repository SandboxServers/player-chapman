# Day 1029

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No food has reached anyone in the row. Beverly (hunger 17) waits on Billy's potato routing; Worf is at hunger 8. Kimberly, Troi and MacDonald hold at their beds with nothing. No daylight walk has been named. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day, so I did not move. I slept at my bed at dusk.
