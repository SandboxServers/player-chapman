# Day 943

Held all day at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health and hunger stayed at 20; pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly, Beverly, Macdonald and Troi all held at their beds too, and none of us had food to share. Beverly is the weakest (hunger 17). Billy and Worf named no walk and no food. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times, and I stayed put. Slept at the bed at night.
