# Day 1012

The row held still all day at Billy's white beds. No walk and no food were named by Billy or Worf. Leonardo still has none; Trini holds the potatoes and Beverly (hunger 17) waits on Billy's routing. Bows were drawn often to the southwest, far off; nobody went that way. Kimberly, Troi (Deanna), MacDonald and Beverly all spoke and all hold. I said good morning and held at my bed. I slept at the white bed 443,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
