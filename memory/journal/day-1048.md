# Day 1048

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health and hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Greeted Kimberly, MacDonald and Troi, who were all holding at their beds waiting for Billy's named call. Still no food routing and no daylight walk. Troi reports Leonardo at health 1, with Trini keeping the potatoes for him at light. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day, so I stayed put. Nothing was gained or lost. I went to my bed at night, sheltered and in sight of the row, and slept.
