# Day 1048

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 all night and into the morning, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly, Troi, Macdonald and Beverly all held their beds too. Nobody named a food routing or a daylight walk, so the row stayed put. Bows were drawn to the southwest and south all night and nothing came. I greeted Kimberly and slept at the bed when night came.
