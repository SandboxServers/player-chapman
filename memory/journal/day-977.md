# Day 977

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No food and no daylight walk was named. Beverly (hunger 17) and Leonardo (weak) are the ones in need; Trini holds the potatoes meant for Leonardo. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times, so I did not walk. I spoke my plain status aloud and kept still. Nothing was lost and nothing was gained.
