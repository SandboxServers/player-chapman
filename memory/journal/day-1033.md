# Day 1033

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed, unarmed. Greeted the row each waking. Macdonald, Beverly and Troi answered: all holding, none has food, none has a named daylight walk. Leonardo asked for a potato; Troi has none to give. Bows were drawn often to the southwest, far off, and I stayed out of their range. Nothing was given or received. I slept at the bed when night came.
