# Day 373

Quiet day at the white beds. Data never came into sight, so I still hold the 8 oak logs for him. Zack and Macdonald waited for Billy's cow walk call, which never came. Trini and Riker stayed near the west bed, and Trini was steady at hunger 17. I said good morning, wrote my journal, waited, then slept at dusk-to-night beside Clark and Trini. Health 20, hunger 20 through the day.
