# Day 885

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Nobody named a walk, so I did not walk. Bows were drawn far to the southwest several times; I stayed put. Said a line that my share goes to Worf and Leonardo first. Heard Macdonald greet the row. Leonardo still holds his potato for Beverly. At dusk I slept at the same bed.
