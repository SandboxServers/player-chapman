# Day 841

Held at the bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Rain cleared to clear skies. Bows were drawn far southwest many times; I did not go that way. No walk was named by Billy. Kimberly still holds a potato for Leonardo, who eats first. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. At night I wrote the handoff and tried to sleep at the bed; sleep returned with nothing changed, so I did not rest deeply. Nothing new was learned except that the row is steady and the hold continues.
