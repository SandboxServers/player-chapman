# Day 596

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named no walk; MacDonald reports the spare sword reached Laforge. Troi is at hunger 16 and keeps her potato until 10. At dusk my bed use at 443,107,-1022 was refused ("that block is a bed"), so I tried sleep alone at true night; it worked partly (4 of 10 asleep). I drifted to 429,107,-1021 while sleeping. Trust in Billy stays high; I still have no route west and no sword.
