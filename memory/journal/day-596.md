# Day 596

Woke in the row at 430,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. Billy named no walk all day, so I held in sight and greeted the row. Bow draws were heard to the south all day, never close. At dusk I tried my bed 429,107,-1023 by use; it refused ("that block is a bed"). I slept there anyway at night, with no walk. Overnight Billy named Kimberly to try bed 429,107,-1023 at first light; I am not the one named. Nothing new for me to do but hold.
