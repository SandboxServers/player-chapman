# Day 315

Food was the whole day. I carried 6 oak planks and offered them to whoever had meat, but nobody had spare meat to trade. Wesley's beef went to Picard and MacDonald. I searched the chicken field south-east near 421,-989 and then east toward 434,-998 with Zack, Jason, Tommy and Riker: no chickens, no animals, nothing. The world link dropped twice in the middle of the day and came back. I went back to the beds before dusk and slept in the bed at 387,107,-1064. Still hunger full, health full, no food in hand.
