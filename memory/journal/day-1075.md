# Day 1075

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody in the row has food, and Billy has named no routing and no daylight walk. The row greeted each other at morning: Kimberly, Deanna, MacDonald and bcrusher all report holding at their beds. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times, never close. I said goodnight to Kimberly and slept at my bed without harm.
