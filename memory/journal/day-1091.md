# Day 1091

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. Greeted the row. Everyone is holding at their beds: Kimberly beside me, Troi, Beverly, MacDonald. Nobody has food yet, and Billy has not named a call. Bows kept sounding far to the southwest all day, so I did not move. At night I wrote the handoff and slept at my bed. Nothing was spent or lost.
