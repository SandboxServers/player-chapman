# Day 1091

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and night, health and hunger full, pack empty. No food has reached the row and Billy has not named a daylight walk or a food routing. Kimberly stayed beside me. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times but nothing reached us. Greeted the row at dusk and morning. Slept at the bed at dusk.
