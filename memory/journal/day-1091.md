# Day 1091

Night at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly stayed beside me. Troi said they will hold at their bed too, with no food yet. No food has reached the row, and no daylight walk has been named. I held, said goodnight to Kimberly, and slept at the bed.
