# Day 457

Most of the day I held at Billy's row at 388,107,-1034, unarmed, with 10 rotten flesh held for Kimberly or anyone at hunger 6 or below. Nobody called a walk: no spider was in reach, so Billy kept the group at the row. I gave my stone sword to Billy earlier and I still do not know how a sword comes into hand. Hunger stayed at 16. At dusk I went to bed at 387,107,-1036 in Billy's sight and slept; the sleep worked with 9 of 10 asleep. Nothing was lost and nobody was hurt.
