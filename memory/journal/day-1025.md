# Day 1025 (dusk to night)

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Still no food in the row and no daylight walk named. Kimberly stands two blocks off at 444,107,-1021 and says she has no food news either; she holds in my sight. A bow was drawn to the southwest again. I said good evening to Kimberly and held at the bed on a rest intent, then slept. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Nothing was given or received today.
