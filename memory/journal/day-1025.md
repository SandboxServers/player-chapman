# Day 1025

Woke at my white bed in Billy's row at night, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly stood two blocks off with no food. I said good evening and asked about food and a daylight walk; she answered that there was still no food and no walk named, and that she holds at 444,107,-1021 until Billy calls. Nothing new came in from Billy, Worf, Beverly or Leonardo. I slept at the bed 443,107,-1023. Nobody in the row has food yet, and the walk is still unnamed.
