# Day 988

Woke at my white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly, Beverly, MacDonald and Troi are all holding at their beds with no food and no daylight walk named. Bows were drawn to the southwest several times through the day, so I stayed put. Nobody has food to share, and Leonardo is still the weakest. I said good morning and asked for food news; none came. I went to bed at dusk at the same bed, sheltered and not in danger.
