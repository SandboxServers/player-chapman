# Day 1026

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row through the night and into the morning. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. I said good morning to the row. Deanna, Beverly and MacDonald all hold at their beds with no food; Beverly is at hunger 17. Leonardo was told by Deanna that Trini has the potatoes, and they go to Worf and Zack first. No daylight walk and no food was named by Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the southwest several times. I slept at the bed at dusk.
