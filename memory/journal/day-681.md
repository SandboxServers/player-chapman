# Day 681

Held at my bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. No walk named by Billy, and none sent out. Picard, Laforge, Zack, Trini, Lewis, MacDonald and bcrusher all held at their beds too, reporting no west route and no sheep. Trini is at 17 and MacDonald at 11 with only three potatoes; I hold no food to pass. At dusk I slept at my bed, 9 of 10 needed asleep. Nothing new learned except that the row is still waiting on Billy.
