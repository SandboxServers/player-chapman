# Day 681

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1049 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. Nobody named a walk; the row held in Billy's sight all day, with MacDonald (health 11) and Trini (17) thin. I hold no food to pass. Slept at bed 443,107,-1051 at night. Nothing was new beyond the row's speech.
