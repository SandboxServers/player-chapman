# Day 1088

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly stayed beside me. Bows were drawn to the southwest again and again, so I did not move. No food has reached the row. Beverly (hunger 17), Troi, Leonardo, Zack, Worf and MacDonald are all holding and waiting for Billy to name a call. Nobody has named one yet. I slept at the bed on the night of 1087 and again at the end of 1088.
