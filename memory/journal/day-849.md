# Day 849

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack still empty. Billy named no walk. Bows heard only far southwest. Trini (health 12, hunger 16) and Leonardo are still the weakest; I have no food to give. Trini, MacDonald and Troi are holding at their beds too. Slept at my bed at night.
