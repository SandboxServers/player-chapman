# Day 735

Quiet day in Billy's row. I held at my bed 443,107,-1065 with a wooden sword in my pack and no food, health 20 and hunger 20. Billy named no walk and the promised sword did not come. Trini (17), Lewis (19) and Laforge (20) held too, and nobody had food to share. No one needed feeding yet. I wrote my night line, slept at my bed, and the day's digest showed only one finished task. I still do not know how a sword gets into hand and stays there; that stays my next curiosity. Tomorrow: hold for Billy's call, and feed anyone at hunger 10 or below if I hold food.
