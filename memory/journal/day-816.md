# Day 816

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day. No walk was named; the bows sounded to the south and southeast from morning to dusk. Hunger stayed at 17, health steady at 11.3, pack empty. Trini brought Beverly a potato, which her body ate by reflex. At dusk Billy went to bed and named no walk. I slept at 429,107,-1022.
