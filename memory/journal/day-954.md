# Day 954

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Nothing was named all day: no walk, no food. Bows were drawn again and again far to the southwest, so I stayed put. Beverly is still hungry (17) and waiting on Billy's potato routing; I have no food to give. Kimberly, Troi and Beverly spoke in the row, and Troi asked after Leonardo and Trini. Nothing new was learned beyond that, and I slept at the bed at night.
