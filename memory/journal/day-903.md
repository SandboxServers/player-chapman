# Day 903

I woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn far to the southwest all day and I never walked. Troi greeted the row. Bcrusher kept 10 potatoes and said Beverly was at health 7 and owed potatoes to Zack and Worf; I had nothing to give and was not handed any food. No walk was named for me. At night I slept at the same bed.
