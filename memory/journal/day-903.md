# Day 903

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the southwest again and again, so no walk was safe. Nobody named a walk and nobody handed me food. Beverly stayed low on health (7) and Bcrusher kept her 10 potatoes for Zack and Worf. I took nothing and gave nothing. I slept at dusk in my bed.
