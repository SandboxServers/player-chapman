# Day 719

Held at my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Wooden sword still in pack, no food. Billy never named a call or a walk. Laforge, MacDonald, Picard and Trini all held at their beds too, with the same hope of a word from Billy. Nothing was passed on since I hold no food. Went to bed at night beside the bed, in sight of the row.
