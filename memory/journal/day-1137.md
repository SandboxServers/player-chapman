# Day 1137

I held at the white bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. No named daylight route came from Billy or Worf, so nobody walked and no food reached anyone. Beverly (health 9) is still the weakest. I said where I stood and asked for a checked cell past the row; Kimberly, Trini, Riker and Beverly are holding too. I am at health 13, hunger 17, empty and unarmed. Slept at the bed at dusk; the handoff is written.
