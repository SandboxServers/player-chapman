# Day 1107

I held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row beside Kimberly all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn far to the southwest again and again, so I stayed put. No food has reached the row. Leonardo is at health 1, and Deanna has none to give him yet. Beverly, Kimberly, MacDonald and Deanna all report steady and still waiting for Billy's routing. I greeted the row in the morning and at night, and I slept at my bed. Nothing new to act on; the goals still hold.
