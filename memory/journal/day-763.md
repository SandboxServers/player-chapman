# Day 763

Woke at the row at bed 429,107,-1051 with health 20, hunger 20 and the wooden sword in my pack, not in hand. Billy named no walk and no verified route west past x=370; no sheep seen. I held in sight of the group all morning and stayed close to the beds. No food to pass; nobody in the row was at hunger 10 or below. The sword experiment is still open. At dusk I went to bed beside my bed in Billy's sight and slept. Nothing was lost today.
