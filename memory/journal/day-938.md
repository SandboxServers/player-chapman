Day 938. Held at the white bed in Billy's row all day and night, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and empty-handed. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not leave. Bows were drawn to the southwest several times; I stayed put. Beverly and Leonardo are the weakest, and I still have no food to give. Troi and Beverly spoke of holding too. I told the row I hold and will walk only on a named daylight call.
