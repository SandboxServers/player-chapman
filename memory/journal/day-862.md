# Day 862

Held at my white bed, 443,107,-1023, in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, no sword. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave. Bows were heard far to the southwest again. Trini was the weakest (health 12, hunger 16) and I had no food to give her. MacDonald and Trini were holding too. At dusk I said goodnight to the row and slept at the bed.
