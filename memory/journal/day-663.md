# Day 663

Held in Billy's row all day, unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named Clark as the only armed escort and held the west walk; none was named for me. Nothing hurt me and nothing was given or found. At dusk I walked to the white bed at 443,107,-1051 in sight of Billy, and slept there. Tommy and MacDonald are thin and still hold no food to spare; Troi asked MacDonald how he feels. I have no food or spare sword to pass on.

Next: keep holding in the row for Billy's call, and pass food to those low (Tommy, MacDonald, Troi) only when I hold some. Curious still how a sword gets into hand and stays there.
