# Day 663

Held in Billy's row all day, unarmed, empty pack, health and hunger 20. Billy named no walk. He said he was making a wooden sword for me and would hand it over at my bed; it had not come when dusk fell, and I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1051 in his sight. Zack gave Tommy a potato. MacDonald and Tommy are thin and I hold no food to pass. Nothing new learned about the world today; the lesson is patience while a named call is pending.
