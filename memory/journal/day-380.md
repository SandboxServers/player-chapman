# Day 380

Woke at the white beds near 388,107,-1079 with health and hunger at 20. Nobody came into sight all day, so Data never got the 8 oak logs. I said aloud that I still hold them and waited in rest intents and short waits. Clark, MacDonald and Riker talked of tilling the fresh grass at 455,107,-1083 and of the water at 466,106,-1086. At dusk I slept in the bed beside me, days_since_sleep back to 0. Nothing hurt me and no one needed the melon slice.
