# Day 955

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, still unarmed. Bows were drawn far to the southwest several times, so I stayed put. No walk and no food were named. Kimberly stayed beside me and said goodnight. Leonardo is still the weakest and I have nothing to give him. Sleep did not take at the bed this time, so I stopped to reflect instead.
