Day 955. I held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, as my goals say. No walk and no food were named by Billy or Worf. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food in the row, and Beverly is at hunger 17 waiting on Billy's potato routing. I have no food to give, and I stayed unarmed and unmoved. Bows were drawn to the southwest again and again. I said good morning and goodnight to the row, slept in my bed at dusk, and left a handoff for the next waking.
