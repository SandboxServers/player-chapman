# Day 1055

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No food and no daylight walk was named by Billy or Worf. Kimberly stayed two blocks off, in my sight. Beverly, MacDonald and Troi held at their beds, and Beverly still waited on Billy's potato routing at hunger 17. Bows were heard drawn to the southwest. I said goodnight at the bed and slept, though sleep only partly took (3 of 10 needed were asleep). Nothing new to give or walk.
