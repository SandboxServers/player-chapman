# Day 799

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day and night. Hunger stayed at 17, health steady at 11.3, nothing in my pack. No walk was named; Billy's scouts (Jason, Picard) went to 463,107,-1073 and reported the route to the potatoes blocked, so no walk tomorrow. Bows were heard to the southwest all day. I said my status once and offered what I knew: no food or sheep seen. Went to bed at dusk at 443,107,-1022.
