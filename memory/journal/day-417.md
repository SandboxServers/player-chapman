# Day 417

Woke at the camp beds near 402,107,-1078 with health 13 and hunger 17. Nobody needing food was in my sight except Laforge and MacDonald, who were both full. I went to the west beds for William, who had been hungry, but he did not come into view. Laforge offered his bread for William, and I told him to keep it unless William showed up. With no one hungry in reach, I kept the 3 wheat and slept in the bed at 401,107,-1078. Nothing was lost today; the main gap is that William has not been found.
