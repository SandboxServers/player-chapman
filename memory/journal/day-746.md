# Day 746

Quiet day in Billy's row. Woke at my bed 443,107,-1065 with health 20 and hunger 20, the wooden sword in my pack, and no food. Nobody named a walk, and no one in the row has a verified route west. Held at the bed all day, watching Trini, Lewis, MacDonald and Laforge keep to the same watch. Nothing was gained or lost. Slept at the bed at night.
