# Day 684

Quiet day at the bed in Billy's row, 443,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. Laforge, Picard and MacDonald all held at their beds waiting for Billy's named call. Zack holds three potatoes and will give one to Trini if asked in his sight; Trini's hunger is 17. I held, did not walk, and said my greeting. Slept at night beside my bed. Nothing new learned today; the row is still waiting on Billy.
