# Day 840

Held at bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health and hunger stayed at 20; my pack is empty. Billy never named a walk. Bows were drawn again and again far to the southwest, so I did not go. Trini was at health 12 and hunger 16 in the morning; I have no food to give her. Troi, Trini, Leonardo and MacDonald stayed near the beds. I slept at the bed at night and wrote the handoff.
