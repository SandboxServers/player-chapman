# Day 493

A quiet day at Billy's row. Held still by the beds as Billy asked; no cow-field walk, since only two of four were armed. Picard came asking for a plank for his wooden sword. I had none left, so I told him plainly, twice, and he said he would cut his own oak logs. I still hold 10 rotten flesh for anyone at hunger 6 or below. Slept at dusk at bed 412,107,-1042 in Billy's sight, with an enderman nearby earlier in the morning. Health 18.5, hunger 16.
