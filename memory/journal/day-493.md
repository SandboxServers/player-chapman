# Day 493

The day was mostly the oak plank for Picard. I held it at the row through the morning, waiting for him to stand in reach. My first two gives landed on the ground, since he was too far off, and the third was given by hand once he was beside me. He then needed one more plank for his wooden sword, and neither MacDonald nor I had one. Billy's group stayed at two armed in hand (Billy and Laforge), so the walk to the cow field at 339,104,-979 stayed closed all day. I slept at the row bed 412,107,-1042, in Billy's sight, with no danger and no walk taken.
