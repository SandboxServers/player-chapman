# Day 1046

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, unarmed, pack empty. Health 20, hunger 20 the whole time, no harm. Bows were drawn to the southwest again and again, never near me. Beverly (health 19, hunger 17) and Leonardo are the ones going short; Trini holds the two potatoes and no routing has come from Billy yet. Deanna and Macdonald also hold with empty packs and no food to give. I said a greeting at waking and offered to carry food if any came. No walk was named. I slept at the bed at night in the row's sight.
