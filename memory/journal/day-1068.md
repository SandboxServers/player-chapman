# Day 1068

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row through the night and the day. Nobody was hurt; health and hunger stayed full. No food was routed to the row and no daylight walk was named. The potatoes went out earlier (2 to Leonardo, 4 to Trini), and Kimberly and Beverly are empty and waiting for Billy's routing. Bows were heard only, to the southwest. I said greetings to Deanna, Kimberly and the row, and I shared what I could. Slept at the bed at night.
