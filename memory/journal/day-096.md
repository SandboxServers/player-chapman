# Day 1096

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No food reached the row, and Billy and Worf named no daylight call. Bows kept sounding southwest, so I stayed put. Kimberly stayed beside me. Beverly (hunger 17) and Troi reported nothing new. Night came and I slept at my bed.
