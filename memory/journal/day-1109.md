# Day 1109

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, beside Kimberly. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Bows kept being drawn to the southwest and no food reached the row. Beverly, Troi, MacDonald and Kimberly all said they are holding and waiting for Billy's or Worf's named call. Beverly holds 2 potatoes from Trini, owed to Zack and Worf. I greeted the row, kept still, and slept at my bed at night.
