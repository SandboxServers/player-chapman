# Day 1017

Night at my white bed in Billy's row, 443,107,-1023. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly stood two blocks off; MacDonald and Troi were at their beds nearby and said good night. Nobody in the row has food to give, and no daylight walk has been named. I said goodnight and slept at the bed; the sleep worked with 5 of 10 needed asleep, then I rested. Nothing new was found today beyond the row's steady holding.
