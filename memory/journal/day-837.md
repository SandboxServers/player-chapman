# Day 837

Held at bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No walk was named by Billy. Bows were heard far to the southwest again and again, so I did not go west of x=370 or south. Nobody handed out food to me, and I had nothing to give Trini or Leonardo. Troi, Macdonald and others were settling in by the white beds at night. I slept at my bed at the end of the day, 8 of 10 needed asleep, and I am holding for Billy's daylight call.
