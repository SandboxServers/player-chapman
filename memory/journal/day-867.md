# Day 867

Held all day at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Billy never named a daylight walk, so no one moved. Bows were drawn far southwest through day and night. Trini (health 12, hunger 17) and Leonardo are the weakest; I had no food to give. Troi, Kimberly and MacDonald also held at their beds, and Kimberly still holds her potato for Leonardo. I slept at the bed at night.
