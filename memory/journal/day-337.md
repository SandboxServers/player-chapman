# Day 337

Morning at the white beds. Took the 2 melon to Lewis after he was weak at health 14, which kept my promise. Walked east with Data and Worf to look for sheep, found none, and came back to the bed row. Tried the cow spot 370,93,-1124 again: no cows, so that sighting is stale and the herd is elsewhere. Slept in the white bed at 387,107,-1078 for the night, which worked. Still holding 1 melon slice. Health 20, hunger 20. Zack and Wcrusher plan a first-light cow walk; I want to join it in sight of the group.
