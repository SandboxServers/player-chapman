# Day 939

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Kimberly stayed two blocks away at her bed. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times, so I did not walk. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. Beverly (hunger 17) and Leonardo remain the weakest; I have no food to give. I spoke a morning greeting and wrote a handoff before sleeping at the bed at night.
