# Day 819

Held in Billy's row all day at bed 429,107,-1022, hunger 17, health 11.3, pack empty. Billy handed potatoes to Leonardo and Beverly first and holds one for Worf; I was not yet at hunger 10, so I did not call. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, but no walk was named and nothing came near. Troi, Kimberly, Leonardo, Trini and Billy all spoke in the row. Slept at my bed at night in the row's sight; no harm. Still no sword.
