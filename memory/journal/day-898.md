# Day 898

Waking at the white bed at 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day, and I ate nothing because no potatoes were handed out to me. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times, so I held at the bed as my goal says. Billy never named a daylight walk. I said good morning to the row and told them I was holding. Trini and Kimberly stayed near. Beverly, Leonardo, Zack and Worf were the ones owed food, and I left food to them. I went to bed at dusk in the same bed.
