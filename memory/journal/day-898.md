# Day 898

Held at the white bed at 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Bows were heard to the southwest again and again. Trini, Kimberly, Leonardo, Macdonald and Bcrusher stayed at their beds, and Beverly is still the weakest. Health and hunger stayed at 20. I said good morning and that I was holding until Billy calls. Slept at dusk in the same bed with no errand running.
