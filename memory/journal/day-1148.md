# Day 1148

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, so nobody walked and no food reached the row. Health stayed at 13, hunger fell from 17 with no change in my pack, which is empty. The row (Riker, Trini, Kimberly, Lewis, Meriwether, Beverly) kept the same stance: wait for a checked route with two armed readers. I said where I stood and offered to carry. Slept at the bed at night.
