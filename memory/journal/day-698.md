# Day 698

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack. No walk was named and none came, so I held through the day with plain waits. Speech from Picard, Laforge and MacDonald came in: all holding, no food except MacDonald's three potatoes, and he is at 16 hunger, not yet at 10. Nothing needed feeding. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at my bed beside Billy's row. The sleep worked with 8 of 10 needed asleep.
