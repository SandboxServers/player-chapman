# Day 784

Held at my bed at 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. No walk was named; Billy did not call. Hunger stayed at 17, above the line of 10 where Lewis's potato is kept for me, so I took no food. Health held at 11.3. The row is steady: Clark, Lewis, Riker, Troi and MacDonald all holding, all with little food. I said my position aloud in the morning. Nothing new was learned today, and the sword question stays open.
