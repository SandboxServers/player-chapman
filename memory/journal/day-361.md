# Day 361

Spent the day at the white beds near 388,107,-1079 with 8 oak logs and one melon slice. Gave one oak plank to Worf earlier. Offered the oak logs to camp builders several times; nobody has come to take them yet. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Went to bed at night beside the beds and slept by the sleep verb. Kimberly was low on health earlier; I offered the melon if she asked.
