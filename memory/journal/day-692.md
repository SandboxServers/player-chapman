# Day 692

Held in Billy's row all day at my bed 443,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout, no food, wooden sword in pack. Billy repeated the rule: hold until someone verifies a west route; feed anyone at hunger 10 or below. No walk was named, and I have seen no verified route west. Slept at dusk beside the bed. Nothing new learned except that the row is still stuck and food is scarce; MacDonald is the thinnest at health 11, hunger 16.
