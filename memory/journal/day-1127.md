# Day 1127 (dusk, clear)

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day, with no food and no named daylight route. Health 13, hunger 17, empty-handed and unarmed. Beverly, Kimberly, Trini and Meriwether held at nearby beds too. Nobody in the row has food yet. I slept in the bed at dusk, with the handoff written beforehand. Nothing new reached me except a neighbour's goodnight.
