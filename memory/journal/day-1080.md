# Day 1080

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row with health 20, hunger 20 and an empty pack. Greeted the row. Nothing came to me all day: no food reached the row, and no daylight walk was named. Heard bows drawn to the southwest on and off. Trini holds 2 potatoes meant for Leonardo, who is at health 1. I had nothing to give. I held at the bed and listened, and slept there at night. Nothing was hurt.
