# Day 802

Held at my bed 443,107,-1022 in Billy's row all day, hunger 17, health steady at 11.3. Billy, Zack, MacDonald and Kimberly all reported no food and no verified route NE or west, so no walk was named. Trini got the potato from Troi. Bows were heard to the southwest through the day and night. I slept at night in my bed. Nothing new was found, and the hold continues.
