# Day 953

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No food or daylight walk was named. Kimberly, MacDonald, Troi and Beverly all held too; Beverly's hunger stayed at 17 while she waited on Billy's potato routing. Bows were drawn far to the southwest and south all day, so I stayed put. Troi asked the row who was hungry; I said I had no food. Slept at the bed at dusk.
