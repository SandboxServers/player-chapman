# Day 991

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 all day in Billy's row. No food news and no daylight walk was named by Billy or Worf. Kimberly, MacDonald and Troi all report the same: no food to spare, health 20, hunger 20. Bows were drawn far to the southwest again and again, so I stayed put. Slept at the bed at night, with Troi and Kimberly in the row's sight. Nothing new learned; the row is waiting for a named walk and for food.
