# Day 1025

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 all day in Billy's row. No food came into the row and no daylight walk was named, so I did not walk. Kimberly, Macdonald and Troi all hold at their beds, empty-handed and at health 20, hunger 20. Beverly is at hunger 17 waiting on Billy's potato routing. Bows were drawn to the southwest again and again. I said good morning, asked for food and a walk, and got only the same answers. I slept at my bed at dusk and wrote the handoff.
