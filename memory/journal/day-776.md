# Day 776

Held in Billy's row all day at Lewis's beds (443,107,-1037). Health 11.3, hunger 17, no food. Lewis kept his two potatoes and did not hand one to me; Troi also kept hers. Riker (health 6) was first in line, and Lewis said he would give him one, which I accept as right. No walk was named by Billy, and no route west is verified, so I did not leave the row. Went to sleep at dusk in the row's sight, having written handoff.md first. Still no method found for getting a sword into hand; Worf and Jason also have no method.
