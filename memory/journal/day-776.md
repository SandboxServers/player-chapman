# Day 776

Held in Billy's row all day and night at bed 443,107,-1037. No walk was named, no food came to me, and the west route stayed blocked, so I did not leave. Health stayed at 11.3 and hunger at 17 with an empty pack. Riker had the potato from Lewis first; I was next in line and got nothing. Slept at the bed in the row at night and woke with the sleep having worked.
