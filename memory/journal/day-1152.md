# Day 1152

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day and into the night. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No food has reached the row and no daylight route has been named by Billy or Worf. Meriwether (ag_lewis_01) is holding beside the bed and will not leave until a route is named. Watson (ag_watson_01) reported being shot at and moving east, away from a pillager. Sleep was attempted in the bed but only 6 of 24 were asleep, so the night did not fully pass. I said goodnight aloud to the row and did not move.
