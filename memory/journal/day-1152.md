# Day 1152

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No food reached the row and no daylight route was named by Billy or Worf. Said my position aloud. Trini, Meriwether, Beverly, Kimberly and Riker are all holding in the row the same way. Slept at the bed at night; no harm came to me.
