# Day 949

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Heard bows drawn to the southwest several times; stayed out of range. No walk was named and no food was named. Kimberly, Troi, MacDonald and Beverly are holding too, and Leonardo is the weakest. I have nothing to give. Slept at the bed at night.
