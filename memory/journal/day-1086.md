# Day 1086

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly stayed beside me. Bows were heard drawn to the southwest twice, so I did not go that way. Troi holds at his bed with no food and walks nowhere. No food has reached the row and no daylight walk has been named. At night I slept in my bed; the sleep worked with 3 of 10 needed asleep. Tomorrow: greet the row and hold until Billy or Worf names a call.
