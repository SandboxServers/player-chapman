# Day 1086

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly stayed beside me. No food reached the row; Leonardo was at health 1 and Beverly at hunger 17, and Billy has not named a route yet. Bows were drawn many times to the southwest, so I did not walk. Troi, MacDonald, Beverly and Kimberly all held at their beds. I slept at my bed tonight.
