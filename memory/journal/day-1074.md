# Day 1074

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No food reached the row and Billy never named a food routing or a daylight walk. Leonardo was at health 1 in the morning; I had nothing to give. Bows were heard drawn to the southwest all day, so I stayed put. Troi, Macdonald, Beverly, Kimberly and Billy all held at their beds, waiting on Billy's call. I slept at night at my bed.
