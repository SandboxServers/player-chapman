# Day 985

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. Said good morning and asked for a daylight walk and food news; none was named. Beverly still waits on Billy's potato routing. Deanna, MacDonald, Troi and Kimberly are holding at their beds nearby. Bows were drawn to the southwest throughout. Nothing new learned; nothing was asked of me that I could do. Slept at dusk at the bed.
