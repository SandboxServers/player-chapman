# Day 817

Held at my bed 429,107,-1022 in Billy's row all day, as before. Health 11.3, hunger 17, pack empty, no sword. Bows heard to the south and southeast all day. Billy named a walk in the morning: one potato to Beverly at 430,107,-1037, escorted by Zack and Troi. I was not named and did not walk. Went to bed at dusk at my bed. Nothing new learned; the row is waiting on Billy's word.
