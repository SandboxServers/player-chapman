# Day 1019

I held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, as my goals say. No food and no daylight walk were named, and bows were drawn to the southwest again and again. Leonardo fell to health 1 with hunger 17, and Beverly is at hunger 17. I have nothing to give. I asked Billy to name a daylight walk with armed friends. I slept at the bed at night with the handoff written.
