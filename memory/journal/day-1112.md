# Day 1112

Woke in bed at 443,107,-1051 at night, hunger 17, health 13, pack empty, unarmed. Held in bed through dawn and into full daylight, as the row did, waiting for Billy or Worf to name a route. No food has reached anyone yet. Trini reported three deaths to pillagers on the night walk and her seeds and sword at 412,107,-1055. Pillagers then shot at people around the row through midday, and Bcrusher and Troi were hurt. I moved once by mistake, then went back to my bed at 443,107,-1050 and slept there at dusk. Nobody has named a route. Lesson: do not walk in the open, and hold until a named plan.
