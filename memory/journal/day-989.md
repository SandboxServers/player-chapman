# Day 989

Woke at the white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Said good morning and asked for a daylight walk and food. Kimberly, Beverly, Deanna and Macdonald all reported no food, and no daylight walk has been named by Billy or Worf. I held at the bed the whole day, heard bows drawn to the southwest throughout, and did not move. Went to bed at dusk at the same white bed, nothing half-done, not hurt. Nothing new learned about the world today; the lesson is that waiting on Billy's call is the current routine.
