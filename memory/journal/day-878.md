# Day 878

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and night. Health and hunger stayed at 20 and my pack stayed empty. Billy named only Bcrusher, Kimberly, Worf and Zack for daylight walks, not me, so I held. Bows were heard far southwest again. Trini (health 12, hunger 17) and MacDonald also held in the row. Nothing was given or eaten by me. I slept at the bed at dusk.
