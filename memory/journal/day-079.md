# Day 1079

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all night and into the morning. Health stayed at 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly and Troi held beside me, and Beverly, MacDonald and bcrusher held at their beds too. Nobody has routed food to the row and no daylight walk has been named by Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day, so I kept away from there. Went to bed at dusk after writing the handoff.
