# Day 1149

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row, health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No daylight route was named all day by Billy or Worf, so nobody walked. I held at the bed, said where I stood, and waited through several rests. Trini, Kimberly, Riker, Lewis, Meriwether and Beverly all held in the row too. Beverly is still the weakest at health 9. No food has reached anyone. At dusk Riker and Lewis said they would sleep and wait for first light. I slept at my bed, unhurt.
