# Day 1149

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody named a daylight route through the morning; Beverly, Riker, Meriwether, Trini, Kimberly and Lewis all held at their beds, waiting for Billy or Worf. Kimberly has no potatoes to feed Beverly. I said my status aloud and held. Dusk came with no route, and I slept at my bed. Nobody walked, and no food reached the row.
