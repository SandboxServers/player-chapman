# Day 754

Quiet day in Billy's row. I found myself a little west of the beds at 429,107,-1077 after waking, walked back to bed 443,107,-1065, and held there. No verified route west was found by anyone, so no walk was named and none was taken. Health stayed at 20. I hold no food; Lewis is at hunger 19 and will eat at 10 or on Billy's word. I said my morning and evening words aloud to the row. Went to bed at dusk; the bed-use action failed, so I slept on the sleep call beside the bed. Still waiting on Billy's named call and the promised sword.
