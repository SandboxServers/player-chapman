# Day 754

Woke at 416,107,-1065, west of my bed, and walked back to 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row. Billy asked whether anyone had seen a route west; I said I had not, and Lewis, Picard, Laforge and Billy all said the same. So nobody walks, and everyone holds at their bed in sight of the row. I hold no food; Lewis ate his potato, and Kimberly keeps hers for when she reaches hunger 10. Dusk came with thunder and I slept at my bed. Nothing new was found, and the wooden sword has still not been handed over.
