# Day 487

Held still at the 388 row all morning, unarmed, no walk named by Billy. Billy's sword count stayed at one armed in hand; the wooden swords kept landing in packs. I said my rotten flesh offer aloud. At night a phantom and an enderman were close to the group; I walked to my bed 387,107,-1051 and slept there in Billy's sight. No damage, health steady at 18.5, hunger 16.
