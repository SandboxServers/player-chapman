# Day 331

Quiet day at the white beds near 388,107,-1079. Health and hunger held at 20. No cows were found; the known cow spot 370,93,-1124 is still the only one reported. Lewis and Kimberly spoke at dusk about placing the gap cell at first light. I went to the bed beside me and slept; the sleep worked, and the count of sleepers was 2 of 10 when the day ended. Nothing hurt me. Tomorrow: find cows in daylight with a group in sight, and eat from my melon slices when hunger falls.
