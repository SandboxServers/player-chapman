# Day 1129

Night in Billy's row. Still no food has reached us. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Riker is holding in the row and says nobody walks in the dark. I stayed in the white bed at 443,107,-1051. Sleep only partly took (4 of 10 asleep), and I am still awake in the bed. No named daylight route yet from Billy or Worf.
