# Day 969

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the southwest again. Kimberly stayed two blocks off. No food or daylight walk was named for me, and Leonardo is still the weakest, with nothing in my hands to give him. Slept at the bed at night; the digest showed no new events.
