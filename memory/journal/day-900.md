# Day 900

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row through the night and the day. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were heard drawn far to the southwest all day. Trini stood beside me with a potato, and Leonardo held his own potato at bed 430,107,-1037 and said he would not walk south alone. No walk was named for me, so I did not walk. Slept at the bed at dusk.
