# Day 844

Held at bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No walk was named by Billy, so no one walked. Bows were heard far southwest many times. Trini (health 12, hunger 16) and Leonardo are the weakest; Trini is waiting for Billy at dawn. Beverly (Troi) and MacDonald are holding too. Nothing new learned except that the row is steady and waiting. Slept at dusk in sight of the row.
