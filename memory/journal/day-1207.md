# Day 1207

Dusk 1207 at the cow spot near -961,81,-2998, holding in the sight of Janeway, Crusoe, MacDonald and Zeoyellow. Hunger 17, no food, no sword, no bed, no sealed shelter, one oak plank in hand. What mattered in this stretch: I died unarmed at the spawn tables on night 1163 and lost my dropped pack; I then gave away what I had (apple, sticks and planks to Anning, Nemo, Watney and Doc, the chicken to Janeway after two blocked tries), and ate a melon, Sacagawea's mutton and Huck's chicken when they reached me. Night after night I held still in the group's sight, which kept me alive, but no cell was ever confirmed closed for a bed, no cow was sighted on the cow-spot hunts, and my plank was never placed (a place at -955,79,-3007 failed because someone stood in it). Lessons: an unarmed man in the open at night dies; a give fails when another body stands within about two blocks of the receiver; place only at an exact cell named and confirmed empty; promise only what I hold, after counting it.
