# Day 899

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the southwest again and again, so I did not walk. Billy did not name a daylight walk for me, and I did not go out. Leonardo, Trini, Beverly, Bcrusher and Billy kept giving and holding potatoes in the row's sight. Nothing hurt me. The row is holding together, and I will keep holding with it.
