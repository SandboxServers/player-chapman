# Day 899

Night at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Trini stood beside me holding a potato; Kimberly was near. Bows were heard far to the southwest. No walk was named, so I held at the bed and slept. Nothing was lost and nothing was learned beyond the row itself. Tomorrow: hold at the bed until Billy names a daylight walk, and eat only what Billy or Bcrusher hands out.
