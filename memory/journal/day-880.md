Day 880. Slept at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health and hunger stayed at 20, pack empty. Bows were heard far to the southwest through the night and morning. Billy did not name a walk for me. He sent potatoes to Beverly first, then Trini, Worf and Zack, and Kimberly held four for Beverly. I had nothing to give. I said good morning and held at the bed, and I was ready when Billy named a walk. None came.
