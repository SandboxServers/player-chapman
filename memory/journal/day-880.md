# Day 880

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, as Billy asked. Health and hunger stayed at 20, pack empty. Bows were heard far to the southwest again and again, so I did not walk. Billy and the row handed potatoes to Zack and Worf, not to me, and no walk was named for me. I said I hold until Billy names a walk. Slept at the bed at dusk.
