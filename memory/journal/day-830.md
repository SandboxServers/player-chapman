# Day 830

Held at the bed in Billy's row all day and night beside bed 443,107,-1023. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout, pack empty, no walk named by Billy. Bows were drawn often far to the southwest and never came near. Deanna, Kimberly, Trini and MacDonald held at their beds too. MacDonald reported three deaths at the south beds, with a sword and a potato lying at 444,107,-1037, which is south and near the bows, so I did not go. Nothing else mattered; I waited for Billy's word.
