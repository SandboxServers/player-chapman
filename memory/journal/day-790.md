# Day 790

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. Billy named no walk: no verified route west, no food known, and food is gone from the row. I said my need aloud and asked where a sword comes from; no answer yet. Hunger stayed at 17, health steady at 11.3. Slept at dusk in sight of Billy. Nothing new found; the row waits on Billy's word.
