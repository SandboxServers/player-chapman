# Day 1120

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day and into the night, with Riker, Trini, Kimberly, Lewis and Beverly. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, and no food reached the row. I spoke once to the row that I would stay till first light. Health stayed at 13 and hunger at 17, pack empty, unarmed. I slept at the white bed 443,107,-1050 and wrote a handoff before resting. Nothing new was learned beyond what the row already says.
