Day 813. Woke at my bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 11.3, hunger 17, no food, no sword. Said good morning to Billy. Billy holds by his bed; no walks while the bow sounds to the south, which it did all day. Beverly (health 3) and Leonardo (health 2) are the weakest; Trini and MacDonald offered potatoes. Worf is still unseen and Billy still holds his potatoes. Kimberly and Troi held steady at health 20. Nothing walked, nothing gained. At dusk Billy, Trini and Beverly went to their beds and I slept at my bed.
