# Day 813

Held at the bed in Billy's row all day, no walk called; hunger stayed at 17, health steady at 11.3. A pillager shot at people in the row at dusk; Bcrusher died and woke at the bed with nothing, and his sword lies at 412,107,-1051. Beverly is at health 1 and was given potatoes by Billy and MacDonald. Worf has not been seen. I slept at 429,107,-1022 in the row; my use of the bed failed, so I used sleep instead.
