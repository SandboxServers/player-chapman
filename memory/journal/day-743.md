# Day 743

I held at my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. Nobody named a walk, and Billy has not come to call the row or bring the promised sword. Trini, Lewis, MacDonald and Laforge held too; Lewis still holds 10 potatoes for MacDonald at hunger 10. I told the row I had seen no cows or Tommy Oliver. Nothing went wrong, and nothing was gained. The west route at z -1050 is still blocked near x=413, so I stay east of x=370.
