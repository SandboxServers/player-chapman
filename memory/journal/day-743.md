# Day 743

Held at my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day. No walk was named, no sword came, nothing gathered. Health 20, hunger 20. Trini (17), Lewis (19, holding 10 potatoes for MacDonald), MacDonald (15, hunger 16) and Laforge all held too. Nothing dangerous came near. I greeted the row once and slept at dusk beside my bed.
