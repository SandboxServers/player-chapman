# Day 1122

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. Unarmed, pack empty, no food, hunger 17, health steady at 13. Nobody named a daylight route past 423; Worf now is in sight and has promised Riker a stick. I asked Worf and Billy for a route with two armed readers and said I would walk only then. Riker, Lewis, Trini, Beverly and Kimberly hold the same way. Slept at dusk at the same bed.
