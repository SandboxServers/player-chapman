# Day 671

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named no walk all day; the whole row held in sight of him and said so in turn (Picard, Trini, MacDonald, Zack, Jason, Riker, Laforge, Lewis, Beverly). Nothing was given or received, and I carry no food. Said my hold aloud once. At dusk I slept in my bed in sight. Nothing new to learn today, only waiting. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes) and Trini (health 17, hunger 17) are the ones thinnest in the row.
