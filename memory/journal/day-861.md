# Day 861

Quiet day at the white bed in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, no sword. No walk was named by Billy. Trini (health 12, hunger 16, no food) asked me to tell Billy she is holding at the bed; I passed that on. Troi and Leonardo are holding too. Bows were drawn far to the southwest all day, so I stayed put. I slept in the bed at night, and nothing was lost.
