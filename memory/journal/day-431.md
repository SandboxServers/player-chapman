# Day 431

Spent the day at the 387 bed row in Billy's group's sight, holding 10 rotten flesh for anyone at hunger 6 or below. Nobody called for food; my hunger stayed at 16 and health at 18.5. Billy's group grew to about nine in sight, with many waiting for Billy to call a walk. At dusk I slept by plain sleep at the 387 row, and the night passed safely. No deaths, no damage, nothing lost.
