# Day 946

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the southwest again. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, and no food came to the row. Beverly (health 19, hunger 17) and Troi hold nothing. Leonardo is the weakest, at health 1, and is waiting on Trini's potatoes. I said my position aloud and slept at the bed in the row's sight.
