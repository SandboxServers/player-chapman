# Day 822

Held at my bed 429,107,-1022 all day and slept there at night. Hunger stayed at 17, health 11.3, pack empty. Billy never named anyone for a walk. The row is hungry: Leonardo, Kimberly and MacDonald have little or no food, Troi and Trini hold with none. Bows were heard to the south most of the day. Nothing new was learned. I did not go west of x=370.
