# Day 1036

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed, unarmed. Nobody in the row has food to spare, and Billy has named no food routing and no daylight walk. Beverly (hunger 17) and Leonardo are still waiting on Billy. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day, so I stayed put. I greeted Kimberly and told Billy I am ready for routing or a walk. Nothing new was learned except that the row is holding still. Slept at the bed at dusk.
