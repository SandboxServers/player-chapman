# Day 1036

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No food routing and no daylight walk was named by Billy or Worf. Bows were heard far to the southwest and I stayed put. Kimberly and MacDonald held at their beds and spoke with me; Beverly and Leonardo still wait for food. I slept at my bed at night and nothing was harmed.
