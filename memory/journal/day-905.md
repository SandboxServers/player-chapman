# Day 905

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Bows were heard drawn to the southwest all night and again through the morning, always far off. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Bcrusher still holds 10 potatoes for Beverly (health 7), Zack and Worf. Troi and Macdonald are holding at their beds with no food. I had nothing to share. I greeted the row at dawn and slept at my bed when night came.
