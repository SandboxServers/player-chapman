Day 987. Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Greeted Kimberly. Nobody in the row has food, and Leonardo fell to health 1, with Trini's two potatoes set aside for him first. No daylight walk was named, so I held at the bed all day, out of bow range; bows were drawn far to the southwest again and again. I had nothing to give and said so. At night I slept at my bed.
