# Day 987

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Greeted Kimberly, Troi, Beverly and MacDonald; all are empty-handed and waiting for a named daylight walk or food from Billy. Bows were drawn to the southwest several times, so I stayed put. No food was found and no walk was named. Slept at the bed.
