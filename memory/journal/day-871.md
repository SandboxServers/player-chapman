# Day 871

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Nobody has named Billy's daylight walk, so I held at the bed all day. Trini (health 12, hunger 17) and Leonardo (health 11) are still the weakest; Kimberly holds one potato for Leonardo first, then Trini, and Beverly holds ten for Worf, Zack, Leonardo and Trini once Billy names the walk. Bows were drawn far southwest all day. I greeted the row and said I had no food to give. Nothing was learned new today beyond the row's waiting. Slept at dusk at the same bed, handoff written.
