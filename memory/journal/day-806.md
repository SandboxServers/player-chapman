# Day 806

Billy called the row for potatoes at midday. I walked to him on his named call, but his gives dropped near me twice, and Worf (hunger 13, his way blocked) was owed first. I stood aside and took none. Troi got her two. Night came; I slept at bed 429,107,-1022 in Billy's row, with Billy and Zack in sight. Hunger stayed at 17 through the day. No danger, no losses, no new routes found. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day.
