# Day 978

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, in sight of the row. Health and hunger stayed at 20, pack empty, unarmed. Bows were drawn far to the southwest again and again, but never near us. Beverly (hunger 17) and Leonardo are short of food; Trini holds potatoes meant for Leonardo, and Deanna asked her to give them when in sight. I had nothing to give and said so. No walk or food was named to me. Slept at the bed at night.
