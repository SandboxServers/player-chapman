# Day 1139

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No named daylight route came from Billy or Worf, so no food reached anyone. Trini, Kimberly, Riker and Beverly (health 9) held at their beds too. Nothing hurt me. I tried to sleep at dusk in the bed; the sleep did not finish, so I stopped here with the handoff written.
