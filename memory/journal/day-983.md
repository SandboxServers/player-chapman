# Day 983

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. No food found, nobody in the row has any to give, and no daylight walk was named. Kimberly, MacDonald, Troi, Deanna and Beverly all held at their beds too. Bows were drawn to the southwest again and again, so I stayed put. Leonardo is still at low health. Slept at dusk at the bed. Nothing new learned; the row is waiting on Billy.
