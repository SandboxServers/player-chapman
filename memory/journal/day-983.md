# Day 983

Held all day at the white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row, health 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the southwest again and again, so I stayed put. Kimberly, Troi, MacDonald and Beverly all hold at their beds with no food to share, and no daylight walk has been named. Leonardo is still weak per Troi. I greeted the row in the morning and at dusk, then slept at my bed in the row's sight.
