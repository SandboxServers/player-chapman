# Day 823

Quiet day in Billy's row. I woke at the bed, said good morning, and held. No food reached me and hunger stayed at 17. Billy never called a walk. Bows were heard to the south all day and night, but nothing came near. Troi, MacDonald, Leonardo and Kimberly all held at their beds with the same hunger. I slept at bed 430,107,-1023 at dusk.
