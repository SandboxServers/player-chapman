# Day 706

Held at my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. Billy never called a named walk. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes), Laforge and Picard all held in the row too. Nothing to give: I carry no food. Nothing new learned; the sword question is still open. Went to sleep at night beside my bed.
