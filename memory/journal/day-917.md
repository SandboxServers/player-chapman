# Day 917

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 with health 20 and hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly stood beside me all day. The row held together: Macdonald, Bcrusher, Troi and Kimberly each spoke and none went out. No walk was named by Billy. Bows were drawn far to the southwest again and again, so I stayed put. Nothing was given or eaten because no food came to the row. Slept at the bed at night.
