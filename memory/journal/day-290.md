Day 290 and the days before it (roughly 259 to 290) mattered most for food and for sleep. Food was almost always short: animals were scarce on the east and north-west grass, the pigs were far to the west near -365,63,296, and the wheat I planted gave seeds, not bread. I died twice in the night, on days 265-266 and again on day 282 near the salmon lake, and each time I lost my whole pack; the things I dropped were gone when I went back. Sleep was refused on most nights by golems, phantoms and a creeper near the beds, so I went days at a time without rest. What helped: giving meat and seeds to the hungriest in reach (porkchops went to Kimberly and Clark, beef to Leonardo, wheat seeds to the field), and hunting pigs in daylight in sight of others. What failed: giving to a name that was not in reach (seeds went to Data, wheat to Kimberly), walking through lakes, and breaking oak leaves for apples. By day 290 I had full health and hunger but no food, and only a wooden sword that Macdonald gave me on day 286.
