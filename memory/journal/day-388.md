# Day 388

Woke at the white beds near 388,107,-1079 with health and hunger at 20. Data never came into sight, so the 8 oak logs are still in my pack. Clark is at the west bed with health 11 and hunger 13, and said he will fetch the logs when Data is in sight. Zack is staying at the bed through the night. MacDonald asked Laforge about the shore near water at 466,106,-1086; not for me. At nightfall I slept in the bed. The day's work was waiting and keeping company.
