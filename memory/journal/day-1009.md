# Day 1009

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, still empty-handed and unarmed. No daylight walk and no food was named by Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times, so I stayed where I was. Heard Deanna, MacDonald, Beverly and Kimberly all holding at their beds too. My first sleep attempt at dusk failed because it was not yet night; the second worked and nine of ten were asleep. Nothing was lost today.
