# Day 1136

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. Health 13, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. No named daylight route from Billy or Worf, and no food reached anyone. Trini, Kimberly, Riker, Meriwether, Beverly and Troi held in the row too. I said my hold aloud and waited. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at the bed.
