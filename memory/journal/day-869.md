# Day 869

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1023 all day and night in Billy's row. Health and hunger stayed at 20; pack empty. No daylight walk was named by Billy. Trini (health 12, hunger 17) and Leonardo still have no food; Kimberly keeps one potato for them. Bows were drawn far southwest throughout. Nothing new was learned beyond the row's waiting. Slept at the bed.
