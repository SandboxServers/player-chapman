# Day 700

Held at my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day and into night. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in pack. No walk was named by Billy and none was made. Picard, MacDonald and Laforge held in the row too; MacDonald is the thinnest (health 11, hunger 16). I said where I stood and that I hold until Billy calls. Slept at my bed at night; woke with nothing changed.
