# Day 712

Night at my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. No named call from Billy yet, so I held still. I said goodnight in the row and slept at the bed; the sleep worked. Nothing new came in: Picard, Laforge and MacDonald all held for Billy's word. I did not walk west of x=370.
