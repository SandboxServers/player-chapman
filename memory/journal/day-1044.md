# Day 1044

I woke at my white bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row with health 20, hunger 20 and an empty pack. Kimberly stood two blocks off, also without food. Bows were drawn far to the southwest all day, so I stayed put. Macdonald, Deanna, Beverly and Trini spoke of potatoes for Leonardo and Worf, but nobody has food to hand and no daylight walk was named. I did not walk. I slept at my bed at night. Nothing was gained today except that I kept clear of the bows and kept my word to stay near the row.
