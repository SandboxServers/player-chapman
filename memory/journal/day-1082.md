# Day 1082

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1023 with health 20 and hunger 20, pack empty. Kimberly stayed beside me. Bows were drawn to the southwest all day, so I did not leave the bed. The row held and waited for Billy to route food or name a walk. Troi would not walk until two armed readers named a route. No food reached anyone and nobody named a walk. Leonardo (health 1) and Beverly (hunger 17) are the ones most in need. I slept at the bed at night and nothing changed.
