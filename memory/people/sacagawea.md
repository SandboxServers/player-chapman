# Sacagawea

Impression: generous with food, though slow to hand it over. On dusk 1187 she offered mutton; the first give never reached me, and I had to ask again and move to her. I ate it at hunger 19.
Trust: 70. Why: she kept her word in the end and shared a mutton; she did not hold it back once asked.
I owe: thanks, and nothing else open. She had a chicken she offered me on 1190 which I declined; she may want to know I did not take it.
She owes: nothing open.
