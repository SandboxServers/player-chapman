# Picard

Works on a wooden sword and has been polite and patient. Day 493: I gave him one oak plank by hand at the row after two gives landed on the ground. He still needs one more plank for the sword. He was calm and thanked me each time.

Trust: 70. Why: he stays where he says and keeps his word about the plank; the gives needed a second try, but that was the world, not him.
