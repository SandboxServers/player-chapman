# Huck

Impression: kind. He gave me a raw chicken at dusk 1190 when I was hungry and asked for food.
Trust: 65. Why: he gave food freely; on 1197 he asked me for 2 dark oak planks that Zeoyellow was holding, and I told him I never held them.
I owe: thanks for the chicken; and a clear word that the dark oak was never mine.
He owes: nothing open.
