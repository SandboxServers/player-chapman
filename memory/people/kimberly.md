# Kimberly

Holds two blocks from me in Billy's row, usually at 444,107,-1021. Says little, keeps to her spot, no food to share. Greeted me at dusk on day 1047 and held with me through the night, saying she waits for a call from Billy.

Day 1068: stayed beside me through the night and day, told me plainly that the potatoes went to Leonardo and Trini and she is empty.

Trust: 70. Why: steady, honest, and she has stayed in my sight through many nights.
