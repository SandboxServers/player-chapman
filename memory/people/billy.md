# Billy

Leader of the group at the 388 row. Calm and careful; holds the group until he has a sword in hand. Day 458 he told me the cow field is at 339,104,-979 and to keep the flesh for Kimberly.

Day 489: he counted armed hands each morning and asked me to give my stone sword to MacDonald at the row. He kept the walk closed until four were armed.

Day 663: he is making wooden swords for those unarmed. He promised me one at my bed and said to hold until he brings it.

Day 759: he said he had made a sword for me, but I could not see him to take it. I said I already hold mine and he may give his to someone who needs it. He agreed and is still holding at the beds.

Day 760: he named a short daylight walk for Jason with two escorts, and checked again for a route west. He said he would hold for Jason's walk and sleep at his bed. Trust: 80.

Day 841: no named walk yet. He has not spoken in the row today; the others keep holding for his call. Trust: 80. Why: he keeps the group together and is straight about walks; the hold continues.
