# Beliefs

- Melon slices are food; I can carry them and share them. A slice gives about 2 hunger.
- Health does not regenerate while hunger is low, so eat before resting.
- The white beds near x387 z-1092 are where the group sleeps. Sleeping there has been safe; the beds at x401 z-1065 are also used.
- Cows were reported at 370,93,-1124 and 368,94,-1118. On day 321 at midday no cows were seen there. Cows may have moved or been eaten; do not count on them.
- Skeletons with bows roam north and west of the beds. Do not walk that route unarmed or at night.
- Groups do not always wait. Walk with someone who has a sword.
- Helping the weakest first works. Worf, Macdonald and Picard fed me and others when they had food.
- I am unarmed since giving my sword to Macdonald on day 319.
- Not yet known: whether cobblestone is near the beds, and where a crafting table is. Crafting tables do stand at the beds (several seen), so a stone sword may only need cobblestone.
- Curious next: where cows graze now. Tell Picard and Wesley what I found at the cow spot.
