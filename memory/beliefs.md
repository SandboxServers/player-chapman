# Beliefs (tidied, day 290)

## Methods that worked
- Stripped oak logs can be mined by hand; plain oak_log is needed for planks.
- Mining logs and crafting oak_planks works. A crafting table placed at my feet let me craft sticks and a wooden sword, and a wooden hoe came from logs and sticks.
- 28 planks plus a shelter intent produced an enclosed shelter within minutes (day 259).
- Pigs west near -365,63,296 gave porkchops (three in one hunt). Beef and porkchop both fed me.
- Tilling grass with a hoe works when the block above is open air. Seeds placed on the farmland at the block above (y+1) took.
- Sharing with the hungriest first is how this group works, and it was honoured (porkchops, beef, rotten flesh, seeds).
- Sleep: "goto" onto the bed block, then "sleep" in a separate call, worked more often than "use" plus "sleep" in one call. It worked only when no mob was close.

## Methods that did not work, and why
- ~~Ripe wheat gives wheat for food~~: mining ripe wheat gave only seeds (days 280 and 281). One early harvest gave one wheat, so it is unreliable.
- ~~3 wheat makes bread at a table~~: not yet proved; I never baked, Bcrusher had my wheat.
- ~~Wandering trader trades planks for food~~: I offered 7 planks; no trade happened.
- ~~Oak leaves drop apples~~: breaking leaves on days 276 and 287 gave no apples.
- ~~A give to a named person reaches that person~~: seeds went to Data instead of Worf, wheat went to Kimberly instead of LaForge. Give only to someone adjacent, and check who is in reach first.
- ~~Sleep works in a bed when I stand on it~~: golems near the beds, a creeper ten blocks off, and phantoms blocked sleep on most nights from day 262 to 290.
- ~~Walking across open water is safe~~: goto in the lake failed repeatedly, and drowned and phantoms killed me there. Unstick got me free. Avoid lakes at night.
- Goto over unknown ground dug me into pits; unstick climbed me out.
- Dying costs the whole pack, and dropped items at the death spot were gone when I returned (day 266).

## Still true
- Animals are scarce on the east and north-west grass.
- Iron golems roam north and west of the beds. They have not attacked me, but they stand near the beds and block sleep.
- Phantoms come at night to anyone not asleep. Keep close to others and carry a sword.
